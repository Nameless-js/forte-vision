### Candidate type

- **id:** `two_clients_cash_mixed` — "Наличные двух клиентов одновременно"
- **Reasoning:** Client 1 handed over cash at 6 s, it was machine-counted (10–22 s), and he walked out at 24–26 s before the cash was stored in the safe/drawer or returned — the operation stayed open with his cash on the desk. At 33 s a second client enters, sits at the desk by 36 s, and from 37 s the cashier accepts and processes his item (handover 37 s, machine 42–48 s) while client 1's bundle remains on the desk at the centre-left for the whole second interaction (visible in every tile 36–50 s) and until the end of the video. That is exactly "second client at the desk while the first operation (with cash on the desk) is not finished". The organisers' label (33 s) coincides with the door entry of the second client, which is the first frame where the mixed-clients condition starts to form.
- **Precise t_event:** **33.0 s** — the second client's body crosses the door threshold (at 32.5 s he is still behind the glass with his hand on the handle; at 33.0 s he is inside). The strict condition "second client at the desk" is met at **35.5–36.0 s** (reaches the desk / sits); a rule that requires seating should use 36.0 s with the 33 s label as the onset.
- **Alternatives considered:**
  - `other` — "client left before the operation was finished / cashier kept an absent client's cash on the desk and started the next operation": client 1 walked out at 24–26 s and the cashier neither stopped nor stored the cash. This is the cause of the labeled situation, but the label sits on the second client's entry, not on the departure, so the mixed-cash category is the better match.
  - `cash_left_unattended` — rejected: the cashier never leaves the desk and the safe stays closed; the cash is on the desk with the cashier present (it is "unattended" only by its owner).
  - `no_id_check` — a plausible *secondary* violation at 6–8 s: client 1 put the cash on the desk and the cashier picked it up and counted it without any document being shown; not the labeled moment. Whether client 2's 37 s handover is an ID or cash cannot be resolved.

  - `accept_without_count` / `issue_without_count` — rejected: the counting machine is used at 10–22 s and 42–48 s; no cash is issued.
  - `cashier_left_during_service`, `phone_at_workplace`, `collection_bag_unchecked`, `handover_bypassing_transfer_device` — nothing of the kind is visible (no tray exists in the scene).
- **Confidence:** 0.8 that the labeled event is the two-clients / mixed-cash situation; the identity difference between the two men (glasses, collar type, hair) is clear at native resolution.
