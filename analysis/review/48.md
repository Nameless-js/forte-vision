### Normal protocol observed

This clean video defines the NORMAL cash-acceptance (приём наличных) protocol:

1. ~2–5 s — client enters through the glass door and sits on the far-side chair.
2. ~8 s — client presents an ID document; ~9–13 s — cashier takes it, examines it at eye level for several seconds, then parks it on the desk for the duration of the operation (ID check BEFORE any cash handling → rules out "Операция без предъявления документа").
3. ~17–18 s — client hands the cash across the desk; cashier receives it.
4. ~21–25 s — cashier counts banknotes BY HAND in front of the client (both hands at chest, leafing motions).
5. ~26–33 s — cashier additionally runs the notes through the black COUNTING MACHINE and retrieves them (полистный пересчёт in the client's presence → rules out "Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта").
6. ~34–46 s — verification/confirmation with the client; cashier handles the counted bundle.
7. ~49–53 s — cashier opens the safe, stores the cash, closes the safe door (cash never left unattended; safe closed before anything else happens).
8. ~55 s — cashier returns the ID card to the client only AFTER the cash is stored.
9. ~56–60 s — client stands up and leaves; operation fully finished.
10. ~62–71 s — only then does the second client enter and present his own ID; the second operation starts from step 2 again (no overlap of two clients' cash).

The cashier remains at her workplace for the entire video.

### Detection cues

- **ID check**: small white card crossing the desk midline client→cashier within ~5 s of the client sitting; cashier's hands raised to face level holding the card for 2+ s (9–12 s, 71 s); card object persisting on the desk near the cashier until the end of the operation; reverse crossing cashier→client at the end (55 s).
- **Hand counting**: cashier's both hands held together at chest height with repetitive small finger/wrist motions for 3+ s (21–25 s, 42–46 s), head tilted down toward the hands.
- **Machine counting**: cashier's arm extension toward the black machine at the left desk end (26–27 s in, 32–33 s out); the machine is a fixed dark box — a stable anchor object.
- **Storage**: cashier's torso rotation to the right toward the grey safe, safe door swing (open ~49 s, closed ~53 s), cash bundle visible in hand before the reach and absent after.
- **Correct ordering**: ID shown → cash received → counted (hand and/or machine) → stored in safe → ID returned → client leaves → next client enters. Any permutation or missing step is the violation signal, not the individual actions.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- 47–53 s: the cashier turns away from the client and opens the safe. A naive "cashier turned away / reached into safe" or "safe open" rule would flag this; here it is the required storage step and the door is closed within ~4 s.
- 62–71 s: a second client is present in the room while the cashier is at the desk. A naive "two clients in one video" rule would fire; in fact the first operation was fully completed (cash in safe 53 s, ID returned 55 s, client gone 60 s) before Client 2 reached the desk — no "Наличные двух клиентов одновременно".
- 6–56 s: Client 1 holds a black phone/wallet-like object the whole time. A phone detector not conditioned on WHO holds it would raise "Использование телефона на рабочем месте"; the cashier never holds a phone.
- 8 s, 17–18 s, 55 s, 69–70 s: hands cross the desk midline for handovers. There is no transfer tray in this scene, so over-the-desk handover is normal; a "hand crossed the counter line" rule must allow brief (<2 s) symmetric handover gestures and only flag deep reaches into the cashier's zone/safe/drawer.
- 26–33 s: the cashier looks away from the client toward the counting machine — normal machine counting, not inattention.
- 59–61 s: the cashier is alone with the desk empty of cash — no "Наличные оставлены без присмотра" because no cash is out (it is in the closed safe).
