### Detection cues

- **Operator-chair occupancy**: no person track overlapping the blue near-side chair / operator ROI for > 3 s → "cashier absent" state. In this clip the state holds for the whole 14.3 s.
- **Restricted-zone polygon**: the far-right corner behind the desk containing the safe, the counting machine and the small device (plus the far-side strip between desk and door up to the safe). Alert when a track enters this polygon while the operator ROI is empty (here 5.5 s), or while a different track occupies the operator ROI.
- **Trajectory**: track originates in the door ROI (4.0–4.5 s) and moves along the far side of the desk to the safe corner within ~1 s (4.5–5.5 s) — a "door → equipment corner" path, not "door → visitor chair → stop in front of the laptop end".
- **Pose/wrist cues**: a wrist keypoint inside the counting-machine / device bounding box for ≥ 2 s (here 9.0–14.3 s, left wrist) by a person whose body centre is not in the operator ROI.
- **Chair displacement**: the far-side chair bbox moves away from the desk (6.5–7.5 s) followed by a sitting pose beside the machine (8.5 s).
- **Second track at the door** while the first track is at the equipment (12.0–14.3 s) — corroborating, not required.
- Appearance is not reliable for the role (the same white-blouse / long-hair appearance is the cashier in other clips); use position (operator ROI vs. far side) and the empty operator chair instead.
