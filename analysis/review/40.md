**Primary: `unauthorized_in_cash_zone` — «Посторонний в зоне кассира».**
Reasoning: the only person in the clip is not the cashier of this set (different clothing, arrives from the client door in outerwear with a personal bag) and no cashier is present at all; she nevertheless acts inside the cash zone: hands on the safe (8 s), personal bag parked against the counting machine (10 s), outerwear across the desk (15 s), takes a chair at the cash desk (16.5–18 s) and from 17.5 s on repeatedly reaches around the counting machine to the small device on the cashier's half of the desk (17.5–25 s). The organisers' timestamp (17 s) coincides exactly with her sitting down at the desk, i.e. the moment a non-cashier establishes herself at the cash workplace; the earlier approach along the far side is the normal client path and the safe touch at 8 s is brief, which explains why the label is at 17 s and not at 8 s. Under the seed definition ("non-cashier enters the cashier's side / client's hand or body crosses to the cashier's side, reaches into the cashier's zone / safe") the reach to the equipment at 17.5 s is the first clear crossing after she sits.
