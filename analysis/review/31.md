### Normal protocol observed

This clip contains no cash operation; it defines the normal "cashier arrives and prepares the workplace" sequence:

1. 3.0–7.0 s — cashier approaches and enters through the glass door (the same door clients use).
2. 9.0–11.0 s — cashier closes the door behind her before going to the desk.
3. 12.0–13.0 s — cashier takes the working seat (white chair, far-left corner of the desk).
4. 14.0–16.9 s — cashier checks the desk surface (hands on the desk, looking down; nothing is picked up, no drawer or safe opened).
5. 17.0–23.8 s — cashier stands and prepares/handles the banknote counting machine with empty hands (touches top, side and front, appears to reposition it); no banknotes are fed, the safe stays closed.

Steps that belong to a cash operation (client seated, ID shown and inspected, cash placed on the desk, banknote-by-banknote or machine count in front of the client, cash stored in the safe, receipt/signature) do NOT occur in this clip because there is no client. Counting observed: none. ID shown: none. Cash direction: none. Storage: none.

Moments a naive detector could wrongly flag (all are normal here):

- 0.0–12.9 s: no cashier at the desk. A "cash_left_unattended" rule keyed only on "desk without cashier" would fire; it must additionally require visible cash on the desk or an open safe — neither is present.
- 3.0–8.0 s: a person enters through the client door and walks into the cashier's side of the desk. A role-assignment that labels "first person through the door = client" would then flag her handling of the counting machine at 17–23.8 s as "unauthorized_in_cash_zone" (Посторонний в зоне кассира). Role must come from behaviour (takes the desk seat, operates the machine) and there is no second person.
- 9.0–11.0 s: cashier stands with her back to the camera at the door (turned away from the desk). A "cashier turned away / left workplace" cue would fire; no client and no cash are present.
- 17.0–23.8 s: cashier stands up and leans toward the right end of the desk, hands near the safe box. Looks like the start of "cashier_left_during_service" or "safe opened / cash_left_unattended". She stays within arm's reach of the desk, the safe stays closed and her hands are on the counting machine, not the safe.
- 18.0–23.8 s: cashier looks down at a black object held in both hands. A "phone_at_workplace" detector could confuse the counting machine (or the static black desk telephone on the left) with a smartphone. The object is desk-mounted, 25–30 cm wide and cabled.
- ~16–23 s: dark silhouettes behind the glass door. A person counter that includes the area behind the glass would report two people and could trigger "two clients" or "unauthorized person". Count only people inside the room (below/right of the door frame).

### Detection cues

- Person count inside the room is exactly 1 for the whole clip (0 for 0–4.9 s). The single person's track starts at the door, goes to the far-left chair, then to the right end of the desk. Role = cashier because the track ends at the desk chair and the hands make contact with the counting machine; no second track ever exists.
- Door state: closed → open at ~5 s → closed at ~11 s. Closing the door before taking the seat is part of the normal arrival.
- Pose: seated 13.0–16.9 s (hip at chair height, torso facing camera); standing/leaning 17.0–23.8 s with hands over the desk at the counting machine. Standing while staying within ~1 m of the desk edge is NOT leaving the workplace.
- Hands: 14–16 s both hands flat on the desk surface, no object in them; 17–23.8 s both hands on the counting machine housing; never any elongated light/greenish rectangle (banknote) in the hands, on the desk or in the machine hopper.
- Counting machine: hand contact without a note stack on the hopper and without the rapid repeated hand-to-hopper motion of feeding; no stacked output in the front pocket. Treat as "machine preparation", not "counting".
- Safe box: no hand contact, no change of its front face for the whole clip.
- Desk surface: the only objects are static (telephone, white object, paper); no new object appears on the desk at any time, so no handover event can be instantiated.
- Durations: entry-to-seat 10 s; seated 4 s; machine handling 7 s to the end of the clip.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- Normal "cashier stands and reaches to the counting machine / safe" (17–23.8 s here, and in cash clips when she switches the machine on or stores counted cash) vs. "cashier_left_during_service" and "cash_left_unattended": distinguish by distance from the desk and by whether cash is on the desk / safe is open while she moves.
- Normal "cashier enters through the glass door and walks behind the desk" (3–8 s) vs. "unauthorized_in_cash_zone": the entering person is the cashier whenever no cashier is already at the desk and the person takes the desk seat; "посторонний" requires a cashier already present plus a second person crossing the desk line.
- Normal "cashier closes the door with her back to the camera" (9–11 s) vs. "cashier turned away from client/cash": no client, no cash.
- Normal "cashier handles the black counting machine while looking down" (18–23.8 s) vs. "phone_at_workplace": the machine is on the desk, cabled and two-handed; the desk telephone on the left is static and should be whitelisted by position.
- Normal "hands flat on the desk, looking down" (14–16 s) vs. "counting by hand": there is no object between the fingers and no repetitive thumb motion.
- Silhouettes behind the glass door vs. a second client: outside the room.
