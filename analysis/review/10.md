### Normal protocol observed

This clip defines the normal pattern "cash is counted on the counting machine, then checked by hand, with the cashier present at the desk the whole time":

1. 0 s — cashier at the workplace; one small cash bundle and one orange item lie on the desk directly in front of her; safe closed.
2. 1–4 s — cashier prepares the banknote counting machine (right hand at its panel / top-right corner); the cash stays on the desk in front of her.
3. 5–6 s — picks up the bundle from the desk with both hands.
4. ≈ 6.5–8.5 s — feeds the bundle into the top hopper of the counting machine (machine count); the bundle is out of sight for about 1–2 s while her right hand stays on the machine.
5. ≈ 8.5–12 s — takes the notes back and handles them with both hands at the desk centre (manual leafing/squaring).
6. 13–14 s — inspects the bundle held in both hands (manual re-check).
7. 15 s — puts the bundle down on the desk in front of her and stays seated. The cash never leaves her reach, the safe is never opened, no document, no client and no phone are involved.

### Detection cues

- **Role assignment must be behavioural, not side-based.** The cashier is the person who operates the black counting machine and sits at the laptop/telephone end of the desk. In this video that person is on the far side, facing the camera.
- **Machine-count event:** the right wrist enters the bounding box of the counting machine's top/hopper region (left end of the desk) and dwells ≥ 1 s while the cash bbox at the desk centre disappears; the cash bbox reappears in the hands within a few seconds. Here: wrist on the machine ≈ 6.5–8.5 s, cash back in the hands by 9–13 s. Treat the machine bbox as a "counting" zone, never as a "storage" zone.
- **Manual check:** both wrists within ~10 cm of each other at the desk centre with a small light-coloured bbox between them for ≥ 3 s and head pitched down (9–14 s).
- **Cash object:** tiny light-blue/white bundle (≈ 25 x 12 px) — detect on the native desk crop (frame rows 27–72 %), track by colour and location continuity; the orange item is a static distractor.
- **Cashier presence:** the cashier's bbox overlaps the desk zone in every frame; no standing (bbox height and hip position constant), no walking; hence cash_left_unattended and cashier_left_during_service are negative for the whole clip.
- **Safe:** closed grey box; no wrist enters its bbox at any time.
- **Client gate:** person count = 1 in every frame; every handover-type category (issue_without_count, accept_without_count, no_id_check, two_clients_cash_mixed, collection_bag_unchecked, unauthorized_in_cash_zone, handover_bypassing_transfer_device) is structurally impossible in this clip.
- **Phone:** no smartphone appears; the only phone-like object is the static black desk telephone next to the laptop — suppress static objects.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- **1–4 s and 7–8 s — reach/lean to the counting machine while cash lies on the desk:** the cashier's hands leave the cash and her torso shifts left; a naive "cash unattended" or "cashier turned away" rule would fire. Require the cashier's bbox centroid to leave the desk zone and/or a standing pose for ≥ N s before firing «Наличные оставлены без присмотра» or «Кассир покинул рабочее место».
- **7–8 s — cash vanishes into the machine:** without a whitelist for the counting-machine bbox this reads like "cash stored without counting" («Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта»). It is the opposite: it is the count itself.
- **0 s and 13–14 s — small light rectangle held in both hands at desk/chest height, head down:** a phone classifier may fire «Использование телефона». Discriminators: light-blue/white colour versus a dark phone, the object lies on the desk among cash before and after, it is fed into the counting machine, and the hands hold it flat (leafing) rather than upright.
- **Static black desk telephone at the right end of the desk:** object-detector "phone" hits in every frame; mask static objects / require the object to be in a hand.
- **Role by side:** the cashier is on the far side facing the camera, so a side-based rule sees "client present, no cashier" and would falsely emit cashier_left_during_service, cash_left_unattended or unauthorized_in_cash_zone «Посторонний в зоне кассира».
- **Orange item on the desk all clip:** if tagged as cash belonging to a second party it could suggest «Наличные двух клиентов одновременно»; a person-count gate (one person only) blocks it.
- **Head turned to the machine (2–4 s, 7–8 s):** "cashier not looking at the cash" is normal here because the cash is in the machine or she is preparing it.
