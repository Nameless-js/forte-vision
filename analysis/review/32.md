### Candidate type

- **Best id: `unauthorized_in_cash_zone` — «Посторонний в зоне кассира».** The cash workplace (desk with the counting machine, safe/cabinet, pedestal) is unattended; a person enters it through the door, goes behind the desk, sits down at it and later handles the counting machine, with no cashier present to authorise or supervise. The organiser timestamp (5 s) coincides exactly with the person having crossed the threshold and arriving at the desk (4.5–5.5 s), i.e. with the moment of entry, which is what this category keys on. The look back toward the door at 12 s and the handling of the machine's hopper/stacker while standing (14–21 s) are consistent with staged "unauthorised access", not with a service operation.
- **Alternative 1: `cash_left_unattended` — «Наличные оставлены без присмотра»** (lower). 0–4.5 s show an open, unattended cash workplace with the counting machine accessible. Against it: no banknotes are visible on the desk; the label sits on the entry at 5 s, not on the empty desk at 0 s; the clip never shows a cashier leaving.
- **Alternative 2: the entrant is the cashier returning** (would make the clip "cashier away from the workplace / workplace left open", `cashier_left_during_service` does not apply because there is no client). Her clothing matches the usual cashier, and the task's role rule ("whoever operates the counting machine is the cashier") would point this way. Against it: she enters through the client door, sits in the far-side (client) chair, never takes a working position, performs no operation, and the label is placed on her entry rather than on an absence. Kept as a real ambiguity (see Uncertainties).
- **Alternative 3: `other`** — "person tampers with / accesses the counting machine while standing, no operation in progress" at 14–21 s; this is a second suspicious moment, not the labelled one.
- Not applicable: issue/accept without count, no_id_check, collection bag, two clients, handover bypassing a tray (no cash, no client, no tray in the scene), phone_at_workplace (no phone in hand).
- **Precise t_event = 4.5 s.** The door opens at 3.5 s and she is in the doorway at 4.0 s (crossing in progress); 4.5 s is the first frame in which she is fully inside the room on the working side, walking to the desk, which is when "a person is in the cash zone without the cashier" becomes unambiguous. ### Detection cues

- Zone polygons on the fixed camera: door zone (top-left), far-side desk zone, machine/safe zone (right end of desk + pedestal + cabinet), near-side cashier seat. Maintain a "cashier present" state = a person track occupying the cashier seat / near side for the preceding N seconds. Fire when a new person track crosses from the door zone into the desk or machine zone while "cashier present" is false (here: false for the whole clip, entry at 4.0–4.5 s, desk zone at 5.5 s, machine zone at 13–14 s).
- Door-state change (leaf angle / brightness change in the door polygon) at 3–4.5 s as the trigger to start the check.
- Dwell: the entrant stays in the desk/machine zones > 15 s with no second person — strengthens the alert; sitting in the far-side chair with no one opposite is itself atypical.
- Pose: upright standing with torso bent forward over the machine box, both wrists inside/adjacent to the machine bounding box for > 3 s (14–21 s), no counting motion, no client.
- Weak cue: head rotation toward the door while seated (12 s).
- Object cues are absent (no cash, no bag, no phone), so this class must be solved with person tracks + zones + cashier-presence state, not with object detection.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- The cashier herself coming back from a legitimate break or arriving for the shift: the visual pattern (door → desk → sits → operates the machine) is identical. Disambiguation needs the preceding context (did a cashier leave?), a uniform/identity rule, or a policy that an open workplace must never be left unattended (in which case the alert is still correct, just re-labelled).
- A client entering through the same door and sitting in the far-side chair while the cashier is at her seat — normal; the discriminator is the empty cashier seat.
- A technician or second employee servicing the counting machine with the cashier present.
- The cashier bending over the counting machine to load/unload banknotes during a normal operation — same pose as 14–21 s but with a client opposite and cash visible.
- Pulling out a chair and sitting (6–9 s) is not a cue on its own.
