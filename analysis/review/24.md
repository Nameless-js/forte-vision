### Candidate type

- **Primary: `cashier_left_during_service` — «Кассир покинул рабочее место до окончания обслуживания клиента».** The entire staged action is the cashier standing up, walking away from the desk and leaving the room while the workplace is still "live" (laptop open, documents / folder on the desk, counting machine and safe in place). The label (8 s) coincides exactly with the moment she opens the door and begins to exit, i.e. the organisers anchored the violation on the "leaves the room" instant. Caveat that lowers confidence: there is **no client** at the desk, so the "до окончания обслуживания клиента" part of the name is not visible; the organisers' real type is probably a generic "cashier left the workplace / workplace unattended" of which this clip is the minimal staging.
- **Alternative 1: `cash_left_unattended` — «Наличные оставлены без присмотра».** The clip can equally be read as "valuables/documents left on an unattended desk": the dark folder/tablet-like object and (presumably) the document she was reading stay on the desk after she leaves. However, no banknotes are visible anywhere, nothing is taken out of the safe and the safe door stays shut, so the "наличные" element is unsupported by what is visible.
- **Alternative 2: `other` — workplace left unattended / unlocked.** Describable as: cashier leaves the cash room with the laptop lid open and work items on the desk, door not locked behind her (she only pulls it shut). This is the literal visible content if neither a client nor cash is required by the organisers' taxonomy.
- **Rejected:** `phone_at_workplace` (the only dark object is tablet/folder-sized and lies on the desk; her hands are empty while walking), `unauthorized_in_cash_zone` (nobody else is in the room; the figure behind the glass never crosses the door line), `issue_without_count` / `accept_without_count` / `no_id_check` / `two_clients_cash_mixed` (no client, no cash, no document handover), `collection_bag_unchecked` (no collector, no bag), `handover_bypassing_transfer_device` (no tray in the scene).
- **Precise t_event = 8.0 s** (±0.5 s). Justification: 5.0 s = rising from the chair (could still be a reach towards the safe); 6.0 s = first frame in which she is clear of the desk and walking away — the earliest "left the desk" cue; 7.5 s = hand on the door handle; **8.0 s = door open and body squared to the doorway — the first frame in which leaving the room is unambiguous**, which is what the organisers labeled; 8.5–9.0 s = crossing the threshold. A detector that fires on "cashier left the desk zone" will trigger ~2 s earlier (≈6.0 s) than the organisers' anchor; a detector that fires on "no person in the room" will trigger ~2.5 s later (≈10.5 s).

### Detection cues

1. **Cashier track leaves the desk ROI.** Identify the cashier as the person whose track originates at the operator seat (here the far-side seat with the laptop; in other videos the near-side seat) and who stays there for the opening seconds. Fire when that track's foot point exits a desk-zone polygon (desk + ~0.5 m margin on all sides, including the safe) and does not re-enter within ~2 s. Here: exit at ≈5.5–6.0 s.
2. **Stand-up signature before the exit.** Bounding-box height jumps by ~40–60 % and the box centroid rises (5.0 s), then the centroid translates horizontally towards the door region (6.0–7.0 s). A stand-up without translation (reaching for the safe) must not fire.
3. **Door interaction.** Hand keypoint within the door-handle ROI (7.0–8.0 s) followed by motion/edge change in the door-panel ROI (7.5–9.0 s). Door opening by the cashier track, as opposed to by an entering person, is strong evidence for the "left the workplace" type and matches the organisers' anchor (8 s).
4. **Room-empty state.** Zero person detections inside the room polygon (exclude everything behind the glass line, which contains reflections) for ≥ 2 s while the desk still shows the laptop/objects: here from ≈10.5 s to the end (4+ s).
5. **Workplace state at departure (for the cash-unattended variant).** Any cash/object detections inside the desk-top ROI at the moment of departure; safe-door state (closed here); laptop-lid state (open here). Record these as attributes rather than hard triggers.
6. **Durations to encode.** Seated phase 0–4.5 s; stand → door 5.0–7.5 s (≈2.5 s); door open → out of room 8.0–10.5 s; unattended desk 6.0–14.7 s (≈8.7 s, clip ends still unattended).

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- **Cashier stands to use the safe or a drawer** and sits back down: stand-up signature present, but the track stays inside the desk+safe zone and re-enters the seat within a few seconds. Require horizontal translation towards the door and a ≥ 2 s absence from the desk zone.
- **Cashier walks to the door to let a client/collector in and returns immediately:** door interaction present, but the track turns back and a second track enters; here nobody enters and she herself goes out and closes the door from outside.
- **End of service / end of shift exit** after the client has left and the laptop/safe are locked: visually identical to this clip; cannot be separated by vision alone (laptop lock state is not visible). The organisers evidently treat the bare "leaves the workplace" as a violation in this staged set.
- **Client or collector leaving through the same door:** a person exiting via the door is normal when it is the client's track. Role must be fixed from the seat of origin — in this video the exiting person started at the operator seat (laptop/counting-machine side), so it is the cashier.
- **Reflections in the glass partition** (seated figure at 2–4 s, standing figure at 8–11 s) can be counted as a second person or as a person "entering"; clip the person detector to the floor area in front of the door line.
- **Brief occlusion of the cashier** by the counting machine when she leans left could look like "left the seat" for a frame or two; use a minimum absence duration.
