### Normal protocol observed

This clip contains no client operation, so the "deposit/withdrawal" steps (ID shown, counting, handover) are absent by design. What it defines is the compliant *leave-the-workplace* sequence:

1. **0–4 s – working at the workstation with a clean desk.** Cashier seated, both hands on the laptop. No cash, no client documents, no bag on the desk; the counting machine is idle.
2. **~5 s – screen state changes before she leaves.** As the cashier rises, the laptop screen changes from bright to dark blue and stays so for the rest of the clip (consistent with locking/idling the workstation; stated as appearance only).
3. **~4.5–6 s – stands up and steps toward the safe** (no client present, so standing is not a service interruption).
4. **7–11 s – operates the safe for ≈5 s** with her body between the camera and the safe front. No cash was on the desk before this and none appears afterwards; nothing is visible in her hands when she arrives at or leaves the safe.
5. **12 s – safe closed, hands empty, turns to the door.** The safe front is visible and flat/uniform before she walks away.
6. **13–15 s – leaves the cash zone through the glass door** with the desk clear and the safe closed.
7. **15–18.3 s – empty workplace with nothing valuable exposed.** This is why the clip is clean despite the cashier leaving: "Кассир покинул рабочее место до окончания обслуживания клиента" requires a client being served, and "Наличные оставлены без присмотра" requires cash out or the safe open — neither condition holds.

Moments a naive detector could wrongly flag (details in the FP section): stand-up at ~5 s, cashier turned away / safe interaction at 7–11 s, unattended desk from 13 s, and the person-shaped silhouettes behind the glass at 0–5 s and 16–17 s.

### Detection cues

Cues an automatic system should use to classify this sequence as normal:

- **Person count inside the room = 1, then 0.** Define the room by the glass-door line; silhouettes behind the glass (0–5 s left edge, 16–17 s top) must be excluded by position (they never cross the door line). Far-side / client zone of the desk: unoccupied for 100 % of the clip.
- **No cash on the desk surface at any time.** The desk ROI (between counting machine and laptop) contains no small light rectangles or bundles in any tile; its appearance at 18 s matches 0 s apart from the laptop screen brightness. A "cash present" state is a prerequisite for both cash_left_unattended and the two no-count categories, so none of them can fire.
- **No handover event.** No second pair of hands, no object transfer across the desk, no reach of the cashier toward the far side.
- **Stand-up event at ~4.5–5.5 s** (cashier bbox height jumps, chair becomes visible) — must be gated by "client present within the last N s"; here the gate is false, so cashier_left_during_service must not fire.
- **Safe interaction 7–11.5 s:** cashier bbox overlaps the safe ROI for ≈5 s, torso toward the safe, both wrists in front of the torso in 8–9 s, back to camera. Followed by a **safe-closed check at 12 s**: safe front visible, uniform grey, no dark door gap, before the cashier bbox moves away. "Safe open when cashier leaves" is therefore false.
- **Hands-empty check at 12–13 s:** no object blob attached to either hand after the safe.
- **Laptop screen ROI brightness drop at ~5 s** coinciding with standing up — a useful (not mandatory) orderly-departure cue.
- **Departure trajectory** desk → safe → door, door crossing at ~14–15 s, desk unattended from 13 s; the "unattended" timer only matters if cash-present or safe-open is true, which it is not.
- **Durations:** seated phase 0–4.5 s; safe phase ≈5 s; departure ≈2 s; empty-room ≈3.5 s.
