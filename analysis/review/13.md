### Candidate type

- **id:** `accept_without_count` — **«Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта»** (here in the variant "incoming cash from a collection bag deposited to the safe without count").
- **Reasoning:** the cash enters the cashier's custody from the collection bag (22 s, 25 s). Between unloading and storing (22–50 s) there is no banknote-by-banknote counting motion (a hand count of a bundle takes 10+ s of repetitive thumb/finger motion with the bundle held in one hand — never seen), and the counting machine at the left end of the desk is never approached (she stays at the right/far corner by the laptop the whole time). The only handling is a ~1 s glance at the green bundle at 44 s. The bundles then go straight into the safe (50 s), which is locked, and the cashier leaves. The organisers' timestamp (47 s) coincides exactly with the cashier at the safe with the uncounted bundles in her hands opening the door — i.e. the moment "storing without count" becomes unambiguous — rather than with the bag opening (12 s) or the unloading (22–25 s), which supports a "no count before storage" reading over a "bag not checked" reading.
- **Alternatives:**
  - `collection_bag_unchecked` — «Инкассаторская сумка без проверки»: a collection bag is opened by the cashier alone, with no collector present, no visible seal inspection beyond a brief look at a tag at 4 s and no accompanying document laid on the desk and compared. Plausible as the organisers' intended scenario ("cash collection handled incorrectly"), but the label time (47 s) is 35 s after the bag was opened, so the labelled moment is the safe deposit, not the bag check. Medium-low.
  - `cash_left_unattended` — «Наличные оставлены без присмотра»: at 36–39 s the two bundles lie on the desk while the cashier steps ~1.5 m to the safe and bends down with her back half-turned; she stays in the room within reach (≤3 s), and at 58–61 s she leaves only after the cash is in the locked safe and the desk is empty.   - `other` — "cash deposited to the safe without being counted on the counting machine / without recount" is the same observable as the primary choice; a procedural "working with a collection bag without a second employee (no dual control)" is not observable as a separate moment.

### Detection cues

- **Context:** exactly one person in the room (no client on the far chair; no second employee), a cloth collection bag held at chest height for 20 s, scissors used at ~12 s (small dark object at the bag neck), bundles appearing on the desk one at a time out of the bag (desk inventory: 0 → 1 at 22 s → 2 at 25 s).
- **Absence of a count event:** no repetitive small-amplitude hand/finger motion over a bundle lasting ≥8–10 s; no approach of the cashier's hands to the counting-machine ROI (left end of the desk); the machine's hopper stays empty the whole video.
- **Storage event:** desk cash inventory drops to 0 (45 s) while the cash appears in the cashier's hands; the cashier's bounding box moves from the desk ROI to the safe ROI (right of the desk) at 45.5–46 s; safe-door opening = a light-coloured rectangular panel appearing on the front-left of the grey safe (48–50 s) and the cashier bending (torso angle) into the safe at 50 s; cash disappears from the hands while at the safe.
- **Rule sketch:** `cash_on_desk` (t0) → no `count_event` → `cash_in_hand` moves to `safe_zone` AND `safe_door_open` within ~30 s → flag at the moment cash reaches the safe zone (here 46 s). Minimum durations: a glance at a bundle (≤2 s) must not count as a count.
- **Role assignment:** derive "cashier" from interaction with the laptop / counting machine / safe, not from which side of the desk the person is on — in this video she is on the far side.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- A cashier transferring bundles that were **already machine-counted** to the safe looks identical at 45–50 s; the discriminator is the presence of a counting-machine or hand-count event earlier in the same operation (none here).
- Putting the **empty bag** or documents into the safe (36–39 s here) is normal and also involves bending at the safe; only cash-in-hand should trigger.
- The brief **inspection of a bundle** at 44 s (both hands, looking down) resembles the start of a hand count; require ≥8–10 s of repetitive motion before accepting a count.
- **Scissors** at 10–13 s and the small light bundle at 44–48 s can be confused with a phone by an object detector; check that the object came from the bag/desk and that the counting-machine ROI is not involved.
- The cashier **leaving the room** at 58–61 s is not "left during service" (no client) and not "cash unattended" (desk empty, safe closed at 51–56 s).
- The short walk to the safe at 36–39 s with cash on the desk is normal within-room movement (≤3 s, within reach), not abandonment.
