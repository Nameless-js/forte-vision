### Candidate type

- **Best id:** `cash_left_unattended` — «Наличные оставлены без присмотра».
  Reasoning: cash is visibly on the desk from 8.0 s; the only person leaves the desk (10.5 s) and then the room (14.5 s) and never returns in the clip; the safe stays closed; nothing is put away. The label at 9 s is the first moment the departure is visible (stand-up onset), i.e. the organisers labeled the *start* of leaving, not the moment the room became empty.
- **Alternatives:**
  - `cashier_left_during_service` — «Кассир покинул рабочее место до окончания обслуживания клиента»: the motion itself (cashier stands up and walks off) is exactly this type, but **no client is present at any time**, so the "during service" condition is not met. ### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- Cashier stands up briefly to reach the safe or the printer and sits back down while cash stays on the desk and she stays within arm's reach of it — the stand-up cue fires but the desk-zone exit does not; require the exit/duration condition.
- Seated torso turn towards the safe or the door (like the 9.0 s pose) without rising — pose yaw alone is not enough.
- Cashier leaving after the desk is cleared (end of operation, cash already in the safe) — requires `cash_on_desk` to be true; the banknote detector must not fire on white receipts, papers, the white pedestal or the white tray of the counting machine.
- A client leaving the desk after a finished operation while the cashier stays seated — the person who leaves is not the one who handled the cash / the desk zone is still occupied.
- In other videos the cashier faces away from the camera; here the only person faces the camera, so a role classifier keyed on orientation would label her a client and treat the departure as a normal client exit.
