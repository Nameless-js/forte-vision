### Normal protocol observed

This clip defines the NORMAL "cashier leaves the workplace with no client present" protocol (end-of-service / end-of-shift securing of the cash zone). No client operation happens, so the counting / ID / handover steps of the other protocols do not apply here.

1. **0–9 s** — cashier alone at the desk, working on the laptop (no client in the room).
2. **10–12 s** — closes the laptop (workstation shut).
3. **13–22 s** — tends to the counting machine / left-end device (clears / switches off / closes it — no banknotes visible at any point).
4. **22–23.5 s** — goes to the glass door and handles / checks it.
5. **24–26 s** — walks around the chair to the safe.
6. **26–32 s** — handles the safe door / lock for ~6 s (secures the safe); stands up; hand on the safe top.
7. **~33 s** — illumination change (consistent with the room light being turned off).
8. **34–36 s** — walks to the door and opens it.
9. **37–41 s** — leaves the cash zone. Desk left with equipment only (no cash, no documents), safe closed, nobody inside.

Key normal-procedure facts a detector must respect: (a) there is **no client** at any time, so leaving the desk is not "Кассир покинул рабочее место до окончания обслуживания клиента"; (b) **no cash is visible** on the desk at any time, so the unattended desk at 33–42 s is not "Наличные оставлены без присмотра"; (c) the cashier is the one who touches the safe, so it is not "Посторонний в зоне кассира".

### Detection cues

- **Person count / tracks:** exactly one person track inside the room polygon for the whole clip; it ends at the door ROI at t≈36. No second track ever enters the room. People faintly visible through the glass partition must be masked out (glass-wall ROI).
- **Role assignment:** must come from behaviour and continuity, not from the side of the desk: the single person who sits at the desk with the laptop and later interacts with the counting machine (t=13–22) and the safe (t=26–32) is the cashier. In this clip she occupies the far-side ("client") chair.
- **Client presence gate:** "cashier left during service" and "cash left unattended" require a client track at the desk (or cash-like objects on the desk) within the same episode; here neither exists, so those rules must stay silent.
- **Desk-surface cash detector:** no small bright rectangular objects (banknotes/bundles) on the desk surface in any frame; the only objects are the two black machines, the laptop (open→closed), the phone and cables.
- **Laptop state change:** open (0–10) → lid moving (11) → closed (12+). Hand on the lid is laptop handling, not a phone.
- **Safe interaction:** hand inside the safe-front ROI for ~6 s (26–32) with the cashier's own track; no door-open appearance; followed by the cashier leaving — this is the normal "secure the safe then leave" pattern.
- **Exit:** track crosses the door ROI at 35–36 s and continues behind the glass.
- **Illumination:** global brightness/colour shift at ~33 s; background models / colour-based detectors should be re-initialised or normalised, and the shift itself must not be treated as an event.
- **Durations:** seated laptop work ~10 s; counting-machine handling ~9 s; walk + safe handling ~9 s; exit ~4 s.
