### Candidate type

**Primary: `two_clients_cash_mixed` — "Наличные двух клиентов одновременно" (simultaneous service of two clients).**
Reasoning: client 1 is in the middle of an operation (his card was presented at 7 s and examined at 8–11 s, it is still lying on the desk, he is still seated and nothing has been returned to him). A second person enters at 17–18 s, comes up to the desk at 19 s and, instead of waiting outside or being asked to wait, is served immediately: the cashier reaches out to him (19.5 s) and takes an item from his hand (20.5–21.0 s), then examines it (22 s). Two clients' items are now on / at the cashier's desk at the same time (client 1's light-blue card on the desk, the second person's white item in the cashier's hands). The staged emphasis is on the cashier accepting the second person's item, which is the "mixing" moment. Caveat: no banknotes are unambiguously visible, so the literal "наличные" part cannot be confirmed; the organisers' real type name may be closer to "обслуживание второго клиента до завершения операции с первым".

Alternatives:
- `unauthorized_in_cash_zone` — "Посторонний в зоне кассира": the second person is not the client being served and walks into the cash room right up to the cashier's equipment (he stands beside the counting machine). He does not, however, cross the counter line to the cashier's side, does not touch the safe, drawer or machine; the whole interaction is a conventional over-the-desk handover. If the organisers treat the entire cash room as the cashier's zone, this id would be the correct one — the visual cue is identical.
- `other` — "cashier accepts an item from a third party / out-of-turn person": generic reading if neither of the above matches the organisers' taxonomy.
- `no_id_check` — the second person hands over his item without showing any document first (his only other object is a dark phone-like item). Weak, because the labeled moment (20 s) is the moment of engagement with the second person, not a cash issue.
- Rejected: `issue_without_count`, `accept_without_count` (no cash identifiable, nothing stored), `cashier_left_during_service`, `cash_left_unattended` (cashier never leaves, safe closed), `phone_at_workplace` (the phone-like object is in the second person's hands, never the cashier's), `collection_bag_unchecked` (no bag), `handover_bypassing_transfer_device` (no tray in the scene).

**Precise t_event = 19.5 s.** The condition "second non-cashier person at the desk while the first operation is open" is already true at 19.0 s (he stops at the desk corner), but 19.5 s is the first frame where the cashier herself acts on it — her right arm is extended across the desk to him with an open hand, i.e. she starts serving him. Here the overlap starts at 18.0 s (second track crosses the door line) and the second track reaches the desk polygon at 19.0 s.
- Second track pose: STANDING (hip–ankle vertical extent, no chair overlap) at the door-side corner of the desk, adjacent to the counting-machine ROI, for >= 1.5 s (19.0–22.6 s).
- Cashier wrist keypoint moving from her body towards the door-side corner of the desk (crosses the left third of the desk box) at 19.5–20.0 s and staying there >= 1 s.
- Wrist-to-wrist proximity between the cashier and the STANDING person (distance below ~30 px at 464 px width) at 20.5–21.0 s = hand-to-hand handover with a non-seated person, while the seated client's track is unchanged.
- Follow-up cue: cashier's wrists near her nose / chin keypoints at 22 s (inspecting a small object) with the newly received object.
- Negative cues that must hold: no cashier stand-up, safe ROI unchanged, no counting-machine activity, no object placed in drawer / safe.
- Duration feature: time from first client's document-on-desk (7 s) to second person at desk (19 s) = 12 s, with no "operation closed" signal in between (client 1 never stands, nothing handed back to him).

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- Sequential service: client 1 stands up and leaves, and the next client enters a few seconds later. Require overlap — the first client still seated / his object still on the desk when the second person reaches the desk.
- A colleague or collector entering while the cashier is idle (no client seated, desk empty) — no open operation, so no violation under this type (may be another type).
- The cashier handing a document or receipt back to the SAME seated client across the desk — same wrist-to-wrist geometry but with the seated track, not a standing second track.
- A single client who stands at the desk end instead of sitting — only one non-cashier track, must not fire.
- Persons walking behind the glass wall at the top-left (visible through the glass at 0–2, 14–18 s) — outside the room; the door line must gate the tracks.
- Cashier reaching to the counting machine with her left arm (12–13 s here) looks like an arm extended towards the door-side corner; distinguish by the absence of a person at that corner and by the arm being the left one towards the machine ROI.
- Client leaning over the desk to place a document at the start (7 s) — brief, from the far side, with only one client present.
