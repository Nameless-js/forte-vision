### Candidate type

- **id:** `collection_bag_unchecked` — **«Инкассаторская сумка без проверки»**
- **Reasoning:** the visitor's object and behaviour (sealed-looking bag with a rigid top bar and tag, standing handover at the desk end, no seat taken, immediate departure) identify a collection-bag delivery rather than a client cash operation. The bag changes hands at 8.0 s with nothing else on the desk: no accompanying document is produced or signed, the cashier does not inspect the tag/seal before accepting, and the collector leaves (turns at 10.0 s, out at 11.0 s) before any check could be done in his presence. The labeled 7 s coincides with the bag being placed on the desk, i.e. the start of the unchecked handover. The cashier's later handling of the bag (9–14.7 s) happens after the collector is gone and without any document, so it does not satisfy the protocol.
- **Alternatives:**

It is a weaker label only because the operation is a bag delivery rather than a client cash transaction; a rule set that treats "no document shown before handover" generically would fire here too.
  - `unauthorized_in_cash_zone` — «Посторонний в зоне кассира»: the man stands right beside the counting machine (cashier's equipment), but he stays at the far/left end of the desk and never crosses to the near (cashier's) side or reaches toward the safe; less likely.
  - `accept_without_count` — «Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта»: the cashier takes the bag and counts nothing, but a sealed collection bag is not counted banknote by banknote at reception, so this reading is a stretch.
  - `cash_left_unattended` — no: the cashier stays at the desk with the bag throughout.
- **Precise t_event:** **8.0 s** (window 6.5–8.5 s). 6.5–7.0 s is the first frame with the bag resting on the desk (the organisers' label); 8.0 s is the first frame in which both the cashier's and the collector's hands hold the bag, i.e. the custody transfer with no document and no inspection; 8.5 s is the release. 8.0 s is the moment a detector can confirm that the bag passed to the cashier without any accompanying check; it is within 1 s of the label.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- **Compliant collection handover:** identical frames until ~8.5 s. The difference lies afterwards: a document appears on the desk and is handed over, the cashier turns the bag and looks at the tag/seal for several seconds while the collector waits, a signing gesture occurs, and only then does the collector leave. Rules must therefore use a post-handover window (≥ 5–8 s) rather than the placement frame alone.
