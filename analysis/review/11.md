### Candidate type

- **id:** `cashier_left_during_service` — "Кассир покинул рабочее место до окончания обслуживания клиента", used in the relaxed sense **"Кассир покинул рабочее место / касса оставлена без присмотра"**.
- **Reasoning:** the only person (the cashier) stands up (11 s), makes final contacts with the safe (16–17 s) and the counting machine (18–18.5 s), then walks away from the desk, opens the door and leaves the room while the workplace remains active (laptop screen on, counting machine on, items on the desk) and nobody else is present. The labeled second (18 s) is exactly the onset of the departure: the last touch of desk equipment before she walks to the door. Important for rule-building: **there is no client in this video**, so the "during client service" qualifier of the candidate definition does not apply — the organisers' type is evidently the broader "cashier left the workplace / cash desk unattended".
- **Alternatives:**
  - `cash_left_unattended` ("Наличные оставлены без присмотра") — correct if the white item in front of the counting machine, or the contents of the counting machine, are cash; not verifiable at this resolution. Operationally the same trigger: cashier absent from an active cash desk.
  - `other` — "cashier takes something out of the counting machine and carries it out of the cash zone": only if the 18–18.5 s contact with the counting machine was a pick-up; her hands are occluded at 19–20 s, so this cannot be confirmed or excluded.
