### Candidate type

**Chosen: `unauthorized_in_cash_zone` — "Посторонний в зоне кассира".**

Reasoning:
1. The labeled second coincides exactly with the man crossing the door into the cash room (12.5 s: behind the glass; 13.0 s: inside). Nothing else changes at 13 s — the cashier's posture and hands are the same as at 12 s.
2. He does not stop on a client side of the desk (in this clip there is no client chair at all); he walks straight to the cashier's chair behind the desk, i.e. into the working zone next to the counting machine and the cabinet/safe, and stays there to the end with his hand on the cashier's chair.
3. The cashier is at that moment occupied with the counting machine and does not stop him; the staged situation reads as "a stranger walks into the cash zone while the cashier is working with the machine".

Alternatives considered:
- `other` — "посторонний в кассовом помещении" (an outsider entering the cash room at all, regardless of the desk side). Functionally the same event; listed separately because the door opens directly onto the cashier's side, so the rule can be "non-cashier crosses the door" rather than "crosses the desk line".
- `cash_left_unattended` — only if the cashier had loaded banknotes into the counting machine at 12–13 s and then turned away to talk (16–18 s). No banknotes are visible and she never leaves the machine, so this is weak.
- `cashier_left_during_service` — rejected: there is no client being served; the cashier only moves from the chair to the machine end of the same desk.
- `phone_at_workplace` — rejected for the label: no smartphone visible; the only phone is a desk telephone at the left end. At 16–18 s her hands are together at chest height but no object can be identified.
- `two_clients_cash_mixed`, `no_id_check`, `accept_without_count` / `issue_without_count`, `collection_bag_unchecked`, `handover_bypassing_transfer_device` — rejected: no cash, no document, no bag, no tray appear in the clip.

**Precise t_event = 13.0 s.** At 12.5 s the man is still outside (only a dark shape behind the glass); at 13.0 s his whole body is inside the room next to the cashier's chair. If the rule requires dwelling in the zone, the condition is clearly met from 13.5–14.0 s (standing behind the cashier's chair) and stays true until the end (18.8 s).

Secondary / out-of-label observations: 0.0–3.0 s the workplace (counting machine, telephone, cabinet) is unattended with nobody in the room; no cash is visible, so this is not a cash_left_unattended event by itself, but a rule based on "no cashier at the desk" would fire here.

### Detection cues

- **Zone definition for this camera placement:** cashier zone = the strip behind the far edge of the desk between the desk and the glass wall, including the white chair, the right end of the desk with the counting machine, and the grey cabinet/safe. The door opens directly into this zone.
- **Person tracking:** identify the cashier track as the person who first sits at the desk / touches the counting machine (here: pink upper body, track starts 1–3 s). Flag any *other* person track whose feet / bounding-box bottom enters the cashier-zone polygon; here a dark-clothed track appears at the door at 12.0–12.5 s (behind glass, partially occluded) and is inside at 13.0 s.
- **Dwell:** the second track stands within ~1 m of the cashier's chair for > 4 s (13.5 s → end), including a hand-on-chair-back pose from 14.5 s.
- **Cashier state:** the cashier is standing, bent over the counting machine (torso tilted, both wrists near the machine bounding box) when the second person arrives — the conjunction "cashier busy with machine + second person in zone" is a strong indicator.
- **Door event:** a door crossing by a non-cashier track while the cashier is already inside (the cashier's own crossing at 2–3 s must not fire).
- **Negative cues:** no hand-over gesture across the desk, no object in the man's hands, nobody on the near side of the desk, the second person never sits.
- Minimal rule: `count(person in cashier_zone) >= 2` OR `(person_in_cashier_zone and track != cashier_track)` sustained ≥ 1 s → event at the first frame it holds (13.0 s).

- Two people standing and talking across the chair without any cash on the desk (16–18 s) looks like a casual conversation; the violation is the location of the second person, not the conversation.
