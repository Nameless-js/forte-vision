### Candidate type

- **id: `other`** — staged as: **«Купюра, отложенная/оставленная счётной машиной, возвращена в пересчитанную пачку без проверки»** (a note the counting machine left in the hopper — i.e. a rejected/suspect or simply uncounted note — is folded, held apart, and merged back into the machine-counted bundle without running it through the detector or the machine again). None of the seed categories describes this.
- **Why this reading:** the clean twins settle it. Video 45 (clean) contains the *same* situation handled correctly: an odd yellow-orange note appears during unloading and the cashier keeps it **separate on the desk** to the end. Video 43 (clean) shows the same deal-notes-down recount with no odd note. Video 44 differs from its clean twins in exactly one visible act — the odd tan note retrieved from the hopper is slipped INTO the counted pack at 16.0 s — and the organisers' label sits precisely on that act. Everything else in the clip (machine count, manual recount, cash left on the desk at the end) also occurs in the clean twins, so none of it can be the violation.
- **Precise t_event: 16.0 s** (±0.5 s window 15.9–16.1). Onset of separation is visible at 15.93 (greens drop, tan retained); the separated tan note is clearest in the raised hand at 15.99–16.03; it merges into the stack by 16.06–16.10. A detector keying on "odd note held apart in a raised hand" should fire at 16.0; one keying on "odd note merged into pack" at 16.1.
- **Alternatives considered:**
  - `other` — «Присвоение купюры кассиром» (pocketing): the fold + brief chest-height hold + 1-frame disappearance of the tan note looks exactly like a palming move, and was my leading hypothesis until the final-stack close-ups (18.4 s, 19.5 s) showed a tan band *inside* the stack on the desk — at least one tan note demonstrably stays in the pack, and her hands stay visible above the desk throughout 16–18 s. Rejected as the primary reading; cannot be fully excluded if more than one tan note was involved (see Uncertainties).
  - `accept_without_count` — «Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта» in a loose "not everything was properly counted" sense: the tan note(s) never pass the machine or the detector, so the pack's machine total excludes them. The mechanics match, but there is no client and no acceptance handover, so the literal category does not apply.
  - `cash_left_unattended` — rejected: the cashier never leaves; the cash on the open desk at the end is identical to the clean twins' endings.
  - `phone_at_workplace` — rejected: the dark object in the raised right hand at 16.0 is note-sized, folded, pinched between thumb and index, emerges from within the cash bundle and ends inside the note stack; no screen, no phone-grip.
  - `issue_without_count`, `no_id_check`, `two_clients_cash_mixed`, `unauthorized_in_cash_zone`, `collection_bag_unchecked`, `cashier_left_during_service`, `handover_bypassing_transfer_device` — not applicable: no client, no second person, no handover, no bag protocol, no tray, nobody leaves.
- **Confidence:** moderate (0.55 on the specific staged meaning; high, ~0.9, that the labeled event is the tan-note separation/merge at 15.9–16.1 s).

### Detection cues

- **Hopper-remainder cue:** after the machine count ends (stacker emptied by hand ~10 s), notes/odd note still present at the hopper top and a hand reaching UP to the hopper to retrieve them (12.8–13.7 s). In clean runs the hopper is empty after counting.
- **Separated-note cue:** one hand holds the pack low (on the desk) while the other hand holds a single folded note raised to chest height for >0.5 s (15.9–16.05 s). Pinch grip (thumb-index), wrist above desk plane by ~15–20 cm.
- **Color-split cue:** two note color classes (mint-green vs tan/brown) tracked as separate blobs; violation pattern = the odd-color blob merges into the main-color stack and never reappears as a separate object; clean pattern (video 45) = the odd-color blob stays separate on the desk.
- **No-verification cue:** between the odd note's retrieval and the pack being laid flat, no contact of any note with the detector device region and no second hopper load.
- **Context gates:** cashier alone (no client at the far chair), counting machine present and recently active, pack on the desk.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- **Dealing notes one-by-one onto the desk during manual recount** — clean video 43 (19–23 s) has the identical "notes drop from hand to desk" motion; alone it is NOT a violation. The discriminator is the retained odd-color folded note plus its merge into the pack.
- **Keeping a rejected/odd note separate on the desk** — clean video 45 (9–27 s): a naive "two note colors on desk" detector fires on the clean clip; require the merge event, not mere co-occurrence.
- **Squaring the stack on its edge near the body** (16.1–17.3 s here and in all clean clips) — hands close to the torso can look like concealment; here both hands stay visible above the desk.
- **Dark clothing artifacts:** her dark-brown vest and the hair strands down her front create static dark blobs at chest height that overlap the raised-hand position at 16.0 s; a per-frame "dark object in hand" classifier will fire on clothing in many frames. Use motion (the note blob moves with the wrist; the vest does not) to disambiguate.
- **Loading the hopper** (3.2–4.5 s) involves the same raised-arm-with-notes pose as the hopper-remainder retrieval at 13.3 s; distinguish by phase (before vs after the machine count/stacker unload).

### Uncertainties

- WHY the tan note(s) stayed in the hopper is not observable (machine rejection of a suspect note, a stop, or a misfeed are visually identical); the machine display is unreadable and there is no audio. The staged meaning "suspect note returned to the pack" vs "uncounted remainder merged without recount" cannot be separated visually — detection logic is the same.
- The number of tan notes (1–3) cannot be counted; if there were two or more, one ending inside the stack (seen at 18.4 s) would not exclude another being retained in the palm during 16.1–18.1 s — the pocketing reading is therefore weakened but not eliminated.
- The tan note's actual insertion into the stack happens on the cashier's side of the standing pack, hidden from the camera; it is inferred from a 1-frame disappearance from the hand (16.03→16.06) plus the tan band inside the final stack.
- The transparent pouch's destination after ~2.8 s is below the camera's view (lap/side); it never reappears.
- Denominations, amounts, screen/display contents: not determinable. The exact violation name used by the organisers is unknown; 16 s unambiguously points at the tan-note separation/merge, and the type is given as the best-supported staged meaning of that act.
