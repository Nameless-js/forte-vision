### Candidate type

- **id**: `accept_without_count` — «Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта»
- **Reasoning**: client 2 hands over a bundle of banknotes at 65–66.5 s. The cashier holds it for only ~1.5 s, performs no banknote-by-banknote count, never feeds it into the counting machine standing directly at her left elbow, and at 68.0 s simply puts the bundle down on the desk and goes back to typing (69.5–72.7 s). The labeled second (68) is precisely the put-down moment that completes "accepted without counting". The first operation provides the in-video contrast: there the cashier visibly handled client 1's beige bundle by hand for ~8 s (33–41 s) before setting it down — the staging shows a counted acceptance first and an uncounted one second.
- **Alternatives**:
  - `two_clients_cash_mixed` — «Наличные двух клиентов одновременно» (strong alternative, almost co-occurring): client 1's beige cash is still lying on the desk, unstored, when client 2 sits (60 s) and hands his own cash; at 68–69.5 s the cashier handles the new bundle in the same spot and the beige bundle stops being separately visible — the two clients' cash is at minimum side-by-side and possibly physically merged. If organisers meant this type, the labeled second also fits (the moment of contact/merge). It is downgraded only because the condition was already true from 60 s, while the label points at 68 s.
  - `cash_left_unattended` — does not fit: the cashier never leaves the desk; the beige cash on the desk is always in her presence.
  - `no_id_check` — does not fit: both clients presented cards that the cashier visibly examined (14–17 s, 61–64.5 s) before cash was handled.
- **Precise t_event**: **68.0 s** (0.5 s resolution). The acceptance completes at ~66.5 s, but the violation becomes visible/decidable at 68.0 s, when the bundle leaves her hand onto the desk after no counting motion; by 70.0 s (hands back on the keyboard) it is fully confirmed.

### Detection cues

- **Hand-to-hand cash transfer event**: client arm extended across the desk + small coloured rectangular bundle changing hands near desk centre (65–66.5 s).
- **Time-in-hand before release**: measure time between the cashier receiving a bundle and the bundle lying on a surface. Here ~1.5–2 s. A genuine hand count looks like 33–41 s of operation 1: bundle held near chest/face, repetitive wrist/finger flicking, at least ~5 s.
- **Counting machine ROI**: the machine occupies a fixed region at the desk's left end; no hand enters its hopper and no bundle is placed on it between 66 s and the end. Machine use would show the cashier's forearm crossing into that ROI plus notes on the hopper.
- **Bundle tracking on the desk**: a bundle object (beige) persisting on the desk across a client change (50–60 s) is itself a red flag; two distinct bundle objects simultaneously on the desk / converging into one blob at 68–69.5 s signals mixing.
- **Posture**: at 68–69.5 s the cashier leans forward with both hands low on the desk arranging items, then straightens and types — "set aside and resume typing" immediately after a cash receipt is the signature of an uncounted acceptance.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- The ID-card handovers (14–17 s and 61–64.5 s) are small-object transfers over the same desk path as cash — colour/size of the object (card vs bundle) must disambiguate.
- Squaring/tidying a bundle for 1–2 s after a proper count (like at 42–45 s, after the 33–41 s hand count) looks identical to the 68–69.5 s handling; the difference is only whether a counting phase of several seconds or a machine pass happened since the bundle was received.
- The cashier's long reach toward the counting machine at 25–27 s and 32 s resembles a client-zone reach but is the cashier's own arm on her own side.
- A client leaning over the desk at greeting (client 1 at ~10 s) can mimic a cash handover or a reach into the cashier zone.

### Uncertainties

- 1 fps limits: the exact instant and direction of the operation-1 cash transfer is not crisply resolved (the beige bundle first becomes visible near the machine; it may have been placed by client 1 at ~10 s or passed around 21–27 s). Direction client→cashier is inferred, not proven.
- Whether the 33–41 s handling of the beige bundle was a true banknote-by-banknote count or merely prolonged handling cannot be verified frame-by-frame at 1 fps, but the duration and posture favour counting.
- Final location of the beige bundle: after 69.5 s it is no longer visible as a separate pile — merged with the blue bundle, hidden under it, or occluded behind the counting machine. It is never seen entering the safe.
- The blue object handed at 65 s is read as a bundle of blue banknotes (flat, rectangular, lies flexibly on the desk at 70–72 s); a phone cannot be fully excluded, but the staging (cash desk, after ID check, placed next to the other cash) makes banknotes far more plausible.
- The small item client 2 carries while walking in (58–59 s) is assumed to be the ID card he then hands over at 61 s.
