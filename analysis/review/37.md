### Normal protocol observed

1. **0–3 s — workplace ready**: cashier seated at the desk, no loose cash visible, safe closed, counting machine on.
2. **1–4 s — client arrives**: approaches behind the glass, opens the door at 4 s, enters alone.
3. **5–7 s — client walks to the desk**; at 6–7 s takes the item out of his trouser pocket (client-side pocket action, not the cashier).

No tray exists in this scene, so an over-the-desk handover is the normal channel here.
5. **10–11 s — manual (полистный) inspection in the client's presence**: cashier holds the notes in both hands at chest height for about 2 s, looking at them; client seated opposite and watching.
6. **12–13 s — machine count in the client's presence**: cashier's right arm goes to the counting machine (right end of the desk), hand at the hopper for about 2 s; display lit; client watches.
