### Normal protocol observed

This clip contains no client-facing operation; it defines the NORMAL back-office handling sequence "cash → counting machine → manual check → safe → paperwork" performed by the cashier alone:

1. 0–3 s — cashier seated at her workplace, cash/item in both hands on the desk (in view the whole time).
2. 4–5 s — cash placed into / machine counting operated at the banknote counting machine (arm into the machine ROI, ~2 s).
3. 6–9 s — cashier waits at the desk with hands clasped (item not in hand; consistent with the machine running).
4. 10–18 s — cashier takes the item back and inspects/leafs through it by hand at the desk centre and at chest height (≈8 s of manual handling, head down towards the hands).
5. 19–20 s — cashier turns to the safe on her left and opens the door (item stays in her hand; nothing is left lying on the desk).
6. 21–23 s — item placed inside the safe (door open ≈3 s, cashier's body next to it the whole time).
7. 24 s — safe door closed; 25–27 s — hand stays on the door front (locking/verification). The safe is never open while the cashier is away or turned elsewhere.
8. 28 s — cashier returns to the desk-facing posture.
9. 29–31 s — cashier takes a document folder from below/beside the left end of the desk.
10. 32–34 s — folder placed on the desk and opened.
11. 35–38 s — cashier reads and fills in / marks the printed sheets (pen-like object), still seated.

Cash is never handed to or received from another person, so none of the handover rules (counting before issuing, counting on acceptance, ID check, transfer device) is applicable; the cashier never leaves; no phone appears; the safe is opened and closed by the cashier herself within ≈4 s of cash being in her hand.
