### Normal protocol observed

This video defines the NORMAL acceptance protocol for the scene:

1. t=3–7 — the client enters, walks to the client side of the desk and takes out a document before anything else.
2. t=8–10 — the document is handed across the desk and the cashier examines it in her hands for ~2 s BEFORE any cash changes hands.
3. t=11 — the client hands the cash bundle across the desk; the cashier receives it hand-to-hand (there is no transfer tray in this room).
4. t=12–14 — the cashier looks at the bundle and feeds it into the counting machine in the client's presence (arm fully extended to the left end of the desk).
5. t=14–21 — the machine counts (~7 s); the cashier waits seated with her hands in her lap, the client watches; nobody leaves.
6. t=21–23 — the cashier takes the bundle out of the machine and checks it.
7. t=24–33 — the cashier opens the laptop and registers the operation while keeping the cash in her hand (it is never left lying on the desk).
8. t=34–42 — she takes the document from the desk, turns to the safe, opens it, deposits the bundle, and closes the door; the safe is open only ~4 s and she stays seated in front of it the whole time.
9. t=43–46 — the client stands up and leaves only after the cash is in the closed safe.
10. t=47–49 — between clients the desk is empty and the cashier stays at her workplace.
11. t=50–57 — the next client enters ~4 s after the previous one has left; the second operation again begins with a document handed over (t=54) and examined by the cashier (t=56–57).

Cash direction: client -> cashier (acceptance) in operation 1; no cash yet in operation 2. Counting: by machine (t=14–21) plus visual inspection of the bundle in the hands (t=12, t=22–23). Storage: safe (t=38–39), door closed at t=41–42.

Moments a naive detector could wrongly flag (all normal here):
- t=35–42 the cashier turns her side/back to the client and opens the safe (looks like "turned away / safe open");
- t=43 client 1 stands and leans over the desk with one hand (looks like a reach into the cashier's zone);
- t=8 and t=54 clients reach across the desk to hand a document (hand crossing the desk midline);
- t=11 hand-to-hand cash transfer over the desk (no tray exists, so this is the only possible way);
- t=9–10 and t=56–57 the cashier holds a small white card near her chest (phone-like pose);
- t=15–20 no hand motion while the machine counts (looks like "no counting" to a hand-motion detector);
- t=24–33 cash held in one hand for ~9 s while typing (cash "out" but attended);
- t=50–57 a second client arrives shortly after the first (no overlap: first leaves t=46, second enters t=50).

### Detection cues

- Person tracks and zones: exactly one person on the near (cashier) side of the desk for the whole video; client tracks enter only through the door at the top-left and stay on the far side; client tracks never overlap in time (client 1 track ends t=46, client 2 track starts t=50). The cashier's track never leaves the chair.
- Document presentation (precondition for "no_id_check" being clean): a small light card-sized object moves from the client's hand across the desk midline toward the cashier (t=8, t=54), followed by the cashier holding it with both hands at chest height, head tilted down, for >= 1–2 s (t=9–10, t=56–57). In operation 1 this happens ~3 s before the cash transfer (t=11).
- Cash acceptance: a light-blue bundle moves from the client's side into the cashier's hands (t=11). It then travels to the counting-machine ROI at the left end of the desk — the cashier's right arm fully extended toward the machine at t=13–14 — and returns to her hands from the same ROI at t=21. Two hand-entries into the machine ROI separated by ~7 s of idle, hands-in-lap posture is the signature of machine counting.
- Storage: chair/torso rotation to the right (toward the safe) at t=35, right arm extended to the safe from t=36, the pale safe-door panel visible as open from ~t=37.5 to ~t=41.5, hands returning empty at t=40, door shut by t=42. The cash leaves the cashier's hands only inside the safe.
- Desk-empty state: nothing lies on the desk from t=42 onward; the desk is empty when the cashier is alone (t=47–49), so no unattended-cash condition arises.
- Phone: no dark hand-held rectangle with the screen facing the face at any time; the only phone-like object is the static landline desk phone at the far edge of the desk.
- Durations that characterise the normal flow: document check ~2 s, machine count ~7 s, laptop entry ~9 s with cash in hand, safe open ~4 s, client departure within ~4 s after the safe closes, inter-client gap ~4 s.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- t=35–42 — cashier turns toward the safe and opens it with the cash in her hands: superficially similar to "cash_left_unattended (Наличные оставлены без присмотра)" or "cashier_left_during_service (Кассир покинул рабочее место до окончания обслуживания клиента)"; distinguishing facts — she stays seated, the safe is within arm's reach, the door is open only ~4 s and is closed before the client stands up.
- t=43 — client 1 stands and leans over the far edge of the desk: similar to "unauthorized_in_cash_zone (Посторонний в зоне кассира)"; distinguishing facts — his hand stays at the far edge, the desk is empty and the safe is already closed.
- t=8, t=54 — clients lean over and reach across the desk with a document: the same crossing-the-midline pattern as an intrusion, but ~1 s long, object-bearing and reciprocated by the cashier's hand.
- t=11 — hand-to-hand cash transfer across the desk: "handover_bypassing_transfer_device" does not apply because there is no tray or barrier in this room.
- t=9–10, t=56–57 — cashier holds a white card-sized document near her chest: a smartphone detector could confuse it with "phone_at_workplace (Использование телефона на рабочем месте)"; the object is white, flat, card-sized and held with two hands while she looks down at it.
- t=12, t=22–23 — cashier holds the bundle and looks at it: could be mistaken for a hand count (harmless either way); the real counting evidence is the machine ROI activity at t=14 and t=21.
- t=15–20 — no hand motion while the machine counts: a hand-motion-only "counting" detector would see nothing and could mis-fire "accept_without_count (Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта)"; the machine events must be used.
- t=24–33 — cash held in one hand while typing on the laptop: not unattended cash; the cashier is present and the cash is in her hand, not on the desk.
- t=47–49 — cashier alone, desk empty: no cash is visible, so no "cash_left_unattended".
- t=50–57 — second client arrives 4 s after the first has left: a "two_clients_cash_mixed (Наличные двух клиентов одновременно)" detector must require temporal overlap of two client tracks (or a second client while cash lies on the desk); here there is none.
- Figures behind the glass partition (t≈1–2, upper-left t≈9–30, t≈48–49) must be excluded by a room ROI, otherwise they look like extra people.
