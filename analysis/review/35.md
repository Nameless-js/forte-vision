### Normal protocol observed
This is a cash **acceptance** (client → cashier) with storage in the safe. Ordered steps:
1. **3 s** — client enters through the glass door; **5 s** — closes it behind him.
2. **6–7 s** — client takes a small light-coloured item from his waist/pocket and holds it in both hands while walking to the desk.
3. **8 s** — client places the light-blue bundle on the desk in front of the cashier (handover across the desk surface, no tray in this room).
4. **9 s** — client sits down facing the cashier and stays seated for the whole operation.
5. **9–11 s** — cashier picks the bundle up and lifts a card-sized light-blue piece to look at it (the only candidate for an ID / first-note check; see Uncertainties).
6. **12–13 s** — cashier loads the notes into the counting machine (right arm to the hopper) — machine count no. 1, in the client's presence.
7. **14–18 s** — cashier works at the laptop while the machine runs.
8. **19–22 s** — cashier takes the notes out of the machine, inspects them (20 s) and touches the machine once more (21 s) — retrieval / second machine pass.
9. **23–28 s** — cashier types; the notes stay on the desk in front of her, in the client's view.
10. **29–36 s** — cashier counts the notes **by hand**, leafing through them with both hands raised above the desk, facing the seated client (полистный пересчёт в присутствии клиента).
11. **37–38 s** — cashier squares / bands the stack.
12. **39–43 s** — cashier turns to the safe, opens it (41 s) and puts the bundle inside (42–43 s).
13. **44–47 s** — cashier turns back to the desk for one second, then closes and secures the safe door (45–47 s).
14. **48–50 s** — cashier finishes at the laptop and passes a small item (receipt / document) to the client.
15. **51–54 s** — client stands, walks out; the cashier remains seated; no cash is left on the desk and the safe is closed.

### Detection cues
- **Cashier presence**: a seated person on the near-side chair, back to camera, inside the desk ROI in 100 % of frames → rules out *Кассир покинул рабочее место* and *Наличные оставлены без присмотра* (cash is never on the desk without her).
- **Client zone**: the client's body stays on the far side of the desk for the whole visit; the only crossing of the desk centre line is a hand/forearm at 8 s that releases an object and retracts within the same second. A rule like "client hand beyond the centre line for ≥2 s, or touching machine/safe/drawer" stays silent here.
- **Handover client → cashier**: a light-blue blob appears on the desk at 8 s at the point where the client's hand stops; the cashier's hands then take it (9 s). Direction = client → cashier.
- **Machine count**: right-arm extension from the cashier's shoulder toward the frame-right machine ROI (hand at the top/hopper) at 12–13 s and 21 s, and at the front/stacker at 19 s; each ~1–2 s. The notes are not visible inside the black machine at this resolution, so the arm-to-hopper event plus the later hand count is the usable evidence of counting.
- **Hand count**: both wrists above the desk plane at chest height, light-blue blob between the hands, repetitive small leafing motion for ≥5 s (30–34 s), cashier facing the seated client. This is the strongest "counted in the client's presence" signature.
- **Storage**: cashier's torso rotates right (39 s), right hand on the safe handle (40 s), grey door blob swings open (41 s), arm inside (43 s), door closed (46–47 s). Open-door interval ≈ 41–46 s, cashier adjacent throughout.
- **Order of events** for a correct acceptance: handover (8 s) → inspection (9–11 s) → machine count (12–22 s) → hand count (29–36 s) → safe (40–47 s) → receipt (49–50 s) → client leaves (51–53 s). Counting precedes storage; storage precedes the client's departure.
- **ID check candidate**: the cashier raising a small card-sized item toward eye level at 10 s. If a dataset rule requires an explicit ID step, this is the only frame that could satisfy it; at 1 fps it cannot be separated from inspecting the top banknote.
