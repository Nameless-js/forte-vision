### Normal protocol observed

This video defines the NORMAL cash-acceptance sequence (приём наличных):

1. **2–6 s — client enters and sits** on the far-side chair; cashier stays seated at her workplace.
2. **8 s — client presents an identity document** (small white card) across the desk; cashier takes it with her hand.
3. **9–13 s — cashier inspects the document** (held up at chest/face height for about 5 s, head tilted toward it) BEFORE any cash changes hands.
4. **14–16 s — document stays on the cashier's side of the desk** (in front of her, left of centre) for the whole operation; cashier glances at the laptop.
5. **17–18 s — client hands over the cash**; the bundle crosses the desk centre from the client's hand to the cashier's hand (no tray in this scene).
6. **19–25 s — manual polistnyi (banknote-by-banknote) count in front of the client** — both hands at chest height, repeated flipping, about 7 s.
7. **26–32 s — machine count**: notes fed into the counting machine at the left end (26–27 s), cashier waits facing the desk (28–31 s), notes taken out of the stacker (32 s).
8. **33–46 s — second manual pass / squaring of the stack**, brief laptop input at 45 s (the amount cannot be read — not described).
9. **47–53 s — storage in the safe**: door opened (47 s), cash placed inside (49–50 s), door closed (51–52 s), hand on the handle/lock (53 s). The safe is open for about 4 s and the cashier is seated beside it the whole time.
10. **54–55 s — document returned to the client** after the cash is secured.
11. **57–60 s — client leaves**; the desk is left with no cash and no document on it.
12. **61–63 s — cashier alone at the workplace** (idle, nothing on the desk).
13. **64–70 s — next client enters and sits only after the first has left**; presents his document at 69–70 s; **71 s — cashier inspects it**. The video ends here, before any cash of the second client appears.

Moments a naive detector could wrongly flag (all are normal here):

- **47–53 s** — cashier turns away from the client to the right, opens the safe, reaches into it, the safe door stands open (48–51 s): looks like "cashier turns away / safe open", but she remains seated at the desk and closes it within ~5 s (`cash_left_unattended` false positive).
- **26–27 s and 32 s** — cashier's left arm leaves the desk centre to the counting machine; the notes vanish from her hands into the machine for 28–31 s. Could be read as "cash disappeared / put away without counting" (`accept_without_count` false positive) — the machine is the counting step.
- **8 s, 17–18 s, 55 s, 69–70 s** — client's forearm crosses the desk midline to hand over / receive the document and cash; **5 s and 68 s** — client's upper body leans over the far desk edge while sitting down. A hand-over-the-line rule would fire (`unauthorized_in_cash_zone` false positive); the client never reaches the cashier's chair, safe or machine.
- **6–56 s** — client 1 holds a dark rectangular object (wallet/card holder) in his hands; a phone detector could call it a phone. It is in the client's hands, not the cashier's — `phone_at_workplace` is about the cashier only.
- **16–54 s** — a small white object (the ID card) lies on the desk for ~40 s, including while the cashier turns to the safe: it is the document, not cash.
- **45 s** — cashier's hands at the laptop keyboard while the counted stack is at her side next to the laptop: not "cash left on the desk", the stack is within her reach and she picks it up at 46 s.
- **57–63 s** — client 1 leaves and the cashier is alone for ~4 s; there is no cash on the desk, so `cash_left_unattended` / `cashier_left_during_service` must not trigger (cashier never stands).
- **60–64 s** — client 1 exits at 60 s, client 2 enters at 64 s; a tracker with ID lag could see them as overlapping (`two_clients_cash_mixed` false positive). There is no overlap and no cash on the desk between them.
- **0–1 s, 6–30 s, 61–63 s, 69–71 s** — people are visible behind the glass partition (top-left); they never enter the room and must be excluded by a room / door-line mask.

### Detection cues

What an automatic system should look for to recognise this correct procedure (and so NOT raise a violation):

- **Workplace occupancy:** the cashier's seat (near side, back to camera) is continuously occupied for all 71.6 s; no standing-up event for the cashier (bounding-box height stays "seated", head stays below the laptop screen top).
- **Document shown before cash:** a small bright rectangular object (credit-card size) passes from the client's hand to the cashier's hand across the desk midline (8 s), then is held at the cashier's chest/face height for ≥3 s (9–13 s) with her head pitched down toward it. The cash handover (17–18 s) happens AFTER this. Order matters: document-handover → inspection → cash-handover.
- **Cash handover direction:** client's forearm extends across the midline with a light flat bundle; cashier's forearm extends to meet it; the bundle ends up on the cashier's side, both of her hands near her chest (19 s). Direction = client → cashier (deposit).
- **Manual count:** both cashier hands held together at chest height, 20–40 cm apart, with small periodic finger/thumb motions and the head pitched down, sustained ≥5 s (19–25 s; repeated at 33–44 s).
- **Machine count:** cashier's left arm extends to the leftmost desk end over the black machine (26–27 s), followed by ≥3 s with both hands away from the machine (28–31 s), followed by the left arm returning to the machine (32 s). Hopper/stacker contents change state.
- **Storage:** right arm extends beyond the right desk edge to the grey box; the safe door changes appearance (open at 48–50 s), the bundle leaves the hand, the door returns to the closed state ≤ 6 s later (52 s) with the cashier still seated.
- **Document return:** the small bright card travels cashier → client (55 s) only after the safe is closed.
- **Client departure and gap:** the far chair becomes empty (57 s), the person track leaves via the top-left door (60 s), the desk has no cash/document on it, and the next person track enters only after that (64 s).
- **No phone:** no phone-shaped object in the cashier's hands at any time; the only phone-like object is in the client's hands.
