### Normal protocol observed

This video defines the normal "secure the cash, then leave the workplace" procedure with no client present:

1. **0–8 s — cash counted by hand in view of the camera.** Leaf-by-leaf manual count (`полистный пересчёт`) of a small stack, seated, hands over the desk, no counting-machine use. Duration ≈7–8 s.
2. **8–9 s — cashier checks the safe location and stands up WITH the cash.** The cash never lies on the desk unattended: it leaves the desk in her hand.
3. **9–11 s — cash carried directly to the safe (≈2 s walk, ≈1 m).**
4. **11–12 s — safe door opened** by the cashier herself while standing right in front of it.
5. **12–13 s — cash placed inside the safe.**
6. **13–14 s — safe door closed** (door open ≈2 s in total).
7. **14–17 s — safe locked**: hand on the lock/handle ≈3 s, then a pull on the handle (lock check).
8. **18–22 s — cashier leaves the workplace only after the cash is secured**; at the moment she crosses the door: client count = 0, cash on desk = 0, safe = closed.
9. **23–26 s — empty room, clean desk, closed safe.**

Moments a naive detector could wrongly flag (all are normal here):
- **t=18–22 s — cashier walks away and exits the room.** A bare "cashier absent from desk" rule would fire `Кассир покинул рабочее место до окончания обслуживания клиента` / `Наличные оставлены без присмотра`. Here no client is being served, no cash is on the desk and the safe is closed, so it is a correct end-of-operation exit.
- **t=9–11 s — cash in hand away from the desk.** A "cash leaves the desk ROI" rule could read this as pocketing; the stack goes straight into the safe within ≈3 s.
- **t=11–14 s — safe open, cashier's back to the camera, body occludes the opening.** Reaching into the safe is the normal storage action; the open interval is fully covered by the cashier standing at the safe.
- **t=1–8 s — small rectangular item held near chest/desk with both hands.** A phone classifier may fire `Использование телефона на рабочем месте`; the item is flexible paper (notes being flipped, one green/teal note visible), manipulated with both hands, no screen glow.
- **t=8 s — head turned away from the desk toward the safe.** Normal orientation before standing up.
- **t=0–13 s — cash goes into the safe without the counting machine.** A rule "cash stored without machine count ⇒ `Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта`" would fire; manual leaf-by-leaf counting precedes storage, and there is no client handing cash, so no acceptance operation exists to be checked.
- **t≈24–25 s — figure behind the glass partition.** Person detection must be restricted to the room's floor polygon, otherwise it reads as a second person / `Посторонний в зоне кассира`.

### Detection cues

- **Person count in the room polygon**: 1 for 0–22 s, 0 afterwards; never 2. With zero clients, the client-dependent categories (`issue_without_count`, `accept_without_count`, `no_id_check`, `two_clients_cash_mixed`, `unauthorized_in_cash_zone`, `cashier_left_during_service`) cannot apply; the only categories that remain testable are `cash_left_unattended` and `phone_at_workplace`, and both are negative.
- **Role**: a seat-position rule would mis-classify her (she sits facing the camera on the far-side chair). Use behaviour instead: sole person + handles cash + operates the safe ⇒ cashier.
- **Cash track**: small light/green rectangular blob at the hand keypoints from t=0; it travels with the right wrist from the desk ROI to the safe ROI (t=9→12) and disappears inside the safe ROI (t≈12–13). After t=13: no cash blob in hands or on the desk ROI for the rest of the video. "Cash disappears at the safe ROI while the safe door is open" = legitimate storage; "cash disappears at torso/pocket ROI" would be the suspicious variant.
- **Safe-door state**: closed → open (≈11.5 s) → closed (≈13.5–14 s); open duration ≈2 s; the open interval is fully inside the interval "cashier within ~1 m of the safe". Hand stays at the lock ≈3 s after closing (locking signature).
- **Exit event**: the cashier's box crosses the door ROI at ≈22 s. At that instant: desk-ROI cash = 0, safe = closed, clients = 0 ⇒ no violation. This is exactly the conjunction that should gate `cash_left_unattended` and `cashier_left_during_service`.
- **Manual-count signature** (t=1–8): both wrists within ~20 cm above the desk, repeated small alternating right→left hand motions at roughly 1 Hz, gaze down, ≈7 s long. Equivalent to counting-machine use for the "counted before storage" condition.
- **Typical durations**: count ≈8 s; stand-up→safe-open ≈2–3 s; safe open ≈2 s; lock ≈3 s; safe→door walk ≈4 s.
