### Normal protocol observed
This clean clip defines the NORMAL pattern "cashier alone at the workplace counting cash" (no client present). Ordered steps:

1. **0 s** — cashier already seated at the desk, alone, holding a small bundle of notes; everything else on the desk static.
2. **1–3 s** — cashier loads notes into / operates the top (hopper/control panel) of the counting machine with her left hand while keeping the rest at desk centre.
3. **4–7 s** — machine pass: cashier waits with both hands at the desk centre, looking at her hands / the machine; display lit.
4. **8–9 s** — cashier removes the counted notes from the stacker and brings them to desk centre.
5. **10–12 s** — second operation at the top of the machine; the other hand keeps the small yellowish bundle on the desk; both hands at centre at 12 s.
6. **13–18 s** — cashier takes the green notes out of the stacker and fans/straightens them in front of the machine.
7. **19–20 s** — further interaction with the machine (hand at hopper/front), eyes on the machine.
8. **21–31 s** — manual banknote-by-banknote recount of the green stack with both hands at desk centre (double-check after the machine), with squaring of the stack at 26 s; the second bundle lies on the desk within reach.
9. **32–33 s** — squared stack placed on the desk directly in front of the seated cashier; hands withdrawn a few centimetres; clip ends. The safe is never opened, no cash leaves the desk, no phone is used, nobody else is in the room.

Moments a naive detector could wrongly flag (all are NORMAL here):
- **1–3, 8, 10–11, 13–20 s** — the cashier's hand/arm enters the right end of the desk, which overlaps the safe/cabinet image region (the machine stands in front of the cabinet). Could be read as "reaching into the safe". The cabinet door never moves.
- **≈9–33 s** — banknotes continuously visible with **only one person** in the room and **no client**. A "cash + no client" or "cash + single person" rule fires "Наличные оставлены без присмотра"; but the cashier is seated at the desk within arm's reach the entire time.
- **4–7 s and 21–31 s** — gaze down, small object held in both hands at chest/desk level for several seconds: looks like "Использование телефона на рабочем месте" to a pose-only detector. The object is a stack of green rectangular banknotes being leafed; no dark slab; the desk phone is never touched.
- **whole clip** — the person handling cash sits in the far-side chair facing the camera, i.e. the seat clients use in other videos. A seat-based role assigner would call her a "client alone with cash" and fire "Посторонний в зоне кассира" or "Кассир покинул рабочее место". Roles must be assigned by behaviour (operates the machine, stays at the desk).
- **≈21–33 s** — a seated person becomes visible through the glass partition. A person detector without a room/door mask may count a "second person" or a "client arriving".
- **32–33 s** — hands leave the stack while it lies on the desk; a "cash on desk with no hand contact for N s" rule could start a timer. The cashier remains seated and looking at the stack.

### Detection cues
- **Cashier presence / no leaving:** a seated-person box overlapping the desk's far-side seat in 100 % of frames; no standing posture, no walking track, nobody crossing the door line. `cashier_left_during_service` and `cash_left_unattended` need the cashier track to leave the desk zone — never happens here.
- **Machine counting:** wrist keypoints entering the machine ROI (right end of the desk): hopper/top ROI at 1–3 s, 10–11 s, 19 s; stacker/front-bottom ROI at 8 s, 13–18 s, 20 s. Machine display is lit in every tile (do not infer the displayed count).
- **Manual counting:** both wrists within a few centimetres of each other over the desk centre with repetitive small-amplitude motion for ≥5 s (21–31 s) while a green rectangular blob (note stack) is in the hands; a squaring motion (hands press the stack flat on the desk) at 26 s and 32 s.
- **Cash attendance:** the green stack blob is always within ≈1 arm length of the cashier's torso; use distance from the cashier's hands/torso to the cash blob, not "number of people in the room", to decide "unattended".
- **Safe state:** the cabinet door region (grey box right of the desk) has no appearance change in any tile — gate any "safe" event on door-region change, not on hands near the region.
- **Role assignment:** the person who operates the machine ROI and stays seated at the desk is the cashier, regardless of which chair she occupies; seat-position heuristics fail on this video.
- **Second-person gating:** restrict person detections to the room polygon (inside the glass partition); the background figure sits outside it.
- **Phone:** no dark slab-shaped object in hand at any time; the black desk telephone box is static for the whole clip.
- **Hand-over / ID rules:** no second person at the desk, so `no_id_check`, `accept_without_count`, `issue_without_count`, `two_clients_cash_mixed`, `collection_bag_unchecked` must not be evaluated at all — they require a client/collector track at the desk first.
