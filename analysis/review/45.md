### Normal protocol observed

This clip defines the NORMAL internal recount procedure (no client involved):

1. **0–1 s** — cashier prepares a banknote bundle in her hands at her workplace.
2. **1–3.5 s** — cashier loads the notes into the counting machine hopper.
3. **3.5–7.5 s** — machine count runs; cashier stays seated, hands visible and clasped on the desk, watching the machine.
4. **7.5–12.5 s** — cashier removes the counted notes from the machine stacker.
5. **12.5–20 s** — cashier checks the notes at the machine's panel; apparent second machine interaction at ≈20 s.
6. **20.5–31.5 s** — cashier performs a manual banknote-by-banknote recount (полистный пересчёт) at the desk — double control after the machine count.
7. **31.5–33.3 s** — cashier aligns the notes into a single stack and keeps it directly in front of her; she remains at the workplace when the clip ends (no storage into the safe is shown within the clip).

Key compliance facts: the cashier never leaves the desk, cash is in her hands or directly in front of her at all times, no phone is used, the safe is never opened, and no outsider enters the cash zone.

### Detection cues

- **Continuous cashier presence**: one person track seated at the desk for the whole clip; no stand-up event, no track leaving the desk ROI while cash is visible.
- **Machine-count signature**: arm raised to the top of the counting machine (hand above shoulder level of the seated pose) for 2–3 s (t≈1–3.5), followed by 3–4 s of a static seated pose with hands clasped (t≈3.5–7.5) — a "load then wait" pattern.
- **Manual-count signature**: repetitive small-amplitude bimanual hand motion over the desk centre at roughly 0.5–1 Hz sustained for ~10 s (t≈20.5–31.5) with a green banknote blob between the hands.
- **Banknote colour blobs**: small green and yellow-orange rectangles on the light wooden desk surface (t≈10–33) — good targets for a colour/texture note detector.
- **Empty client chair**: the teal chair ROI contains no person for the entire clip → no service episode is in progress, so client-dependent rules (ID check, counting in front of the client, two clients) must be gated OFF.
- **Mask the glass partition**: the person behind the glass (t≈21+) sits in a different room; person-in-cash-zone logic must use a floor-level zone polygon inside the room, not the whole frame.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- **t≈1–3.5, 7.5–9, 20 s — reaching toward the counting machine**: the machine stands at the desk end nearest the safe/cabinet; a coarse "hand toward safe" rule could misfire (→ false safe-related flags such as «Наличные оставлены без присмотра»). The safe door never opens.
- **t≈20.5–31.5 s — small object held in both hands**: at 1 fps the green notes in her hands could be misclassified as a smartphone (→ false «Использование телефона на рабочем месте»). Disambiguate by colour, by the leafing motion, and by the desk phone remaining untouched.
- **t≈31.5–33.3 s — cash lying on the desk with no client**: a detector keyed on "cash out + no transaction partner" might flag «Наличные оставлены без присмотра», but the cashier is seated right behind the stack the whole time; unattended requires cashier absence.
- **t≈21–33 s — person behind the glass**: could be misread as «Посторонний в зоне кассира» or a second client; they never cross the door line.
- **Whole clip — no ID, no client-facing count**: «Операция без предъявления документа» and «Приём/выдача наличных без пересчёта» do not apply because there is no client; those rules must trigger only inside a client-present episode.
