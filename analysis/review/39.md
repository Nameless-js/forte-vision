### Detection cues

- Cashier presence: the white-sweater silhouette on the near-side chair is continuous for 0–78.7 s; hips never leave the seat (rules out "Кассир покинул рабочее место").
- Client entry/exit: person track crossing the door region (top-left) inward at 2–3 s and 45–46 s, outward at 38 s and 76 s; seated pose at the far chair 8–35 s and 51–73 s.
- ID check: card-sized object in the cashier's hands at chest height with head tilted down for 2–3 s (8–10 s; less distinct 52–55 s), occurring BEFORE the counting-machine activity.
- Handover: client's wrist crosses the far desk edge and stops near the desk centre line; cashier's wrist moves to meet it within ~1 s (7 s, 12 s, 51 s). The client's hand never passes the cashier's edge of the desk, never approaches the safe, the machine or the pedestal.
- Counting: cashier's right wrist above the counting-machine hopper ROI (top of the machine at the RIGHT end of the desk in this video) for 12–20 s (~8 s) and 56–58 s (~3 s), then at the stacker ROI (lower front) at 23/27 s and 62–63 s. Machine use must count as "пересчёт" — no leaf-by-leaf hand motion is resolvable at 1 fps.
- Storage: right arm to the safe-door ROI -> door-open state (door panel visible swung out towards the desk) -> arm inside -> door closed -> hand on the front: 29–37 s and 65–72 s. Storage follows counting by ~9 s (count end 20 s -> safe open 30 s; count end 58 s -> safe open 66 s), with the client still seated during the whole safe cycle.
- Sequence gating: for each client, order is enter -> handover -> ID at chest -> hopper activity -> stacker -> safe open/close -> client stands -> exits. A violation detector should require this order to be broken, not just any single cue.
- Two-client check: client 1 exits the door at 38 s; client 2 appears at the door at 45 s; no cash on the desk in 38–45 s (it was stored at 32–33 s).

- 34 s and 72 s: client leans forward over the far desk edge to pick up his document / to stand — same FP risk.
- 29–37 s and 65–72 s: cashier turns ~90° to the right, away from the client, with the safe door open for ~4–5 s — not leaving the workplace and not "Наличные оставлены без присмотра"; she stays seated and the client is in view.
- 31 s and 68 s: cashier holds the counted bundle with both hands against her torso for ~1 s before placing it in the safe — a "cash to body / pocket" heuristic would misfire; the bundle goes into the safe in the next second.
- 36–37 s: cashier's hand still on the safe after the client has stood up — the door is already closed (locking), not an open safe during departure.
- 38–45 s: cashier alone at the desk with papers on it — no cash visible; the cashier is present, so no unattended-cash event.
- 45 s: second person entering while the cashier is "busy" at the desk — the first client left 7 s earlier; not "Наличные двух клиентов одновременно".
- Black desk telephone next to the laptop, and the small items in the clients' hands at 5–6 s and 49–50 s — can be mistaken for a smartphone; the cashier never holds a phone.

- Counting-machine use with no visible hand counting (12–20 s, 56–58 s) — a detector that demands banknote-by-banknote finger motion would wrongly produce "Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта".
