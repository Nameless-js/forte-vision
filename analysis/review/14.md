### Normal protocol observed

This video defines the normal, unassisted post-collection processing of a sealed cash bag by the cashier (the collector handover itself is not on tape):

1. 0-7 s — bag inspection: cashier lifts the sealed bag and examines the closure/seal strip in both hands before opening it.
2. 8-27 s — controlled opening: the seal is cut with a hand tool (8-15 s), the tool is put down (16 s), the closure strip is removed and the mouth opened (17-27 s). The bag is held over the desk the whole time.
3. 28-34 s — contents removed in view of the camera: bag opened wide, looked into, two banded bundles tipped onto the desk (31-33 s), second look inside (34 s).
4. 35-39 s — empty-bag check: bag turned upside-down / inside-out and inspected.
5. 40-42 s — the empty bag is stowed beside/at the cabinet; the cash remains on the desk within arm's reach while the cashier stays at the desk.
6. 45-48 s — cashier sits down at the workplace and powers/operates the counting machine.
7. 49-83 s — machine count: bundles unbanded and run through the counting machine in several passes (hands at the hopper 52-54 s, 63-65 s, 75-76 s; notes taken from the stacker 55 s, 66 s, 104 s), with sorting/restacking on the desk between passes.
8. 84-91 s — manual sheet-by-sheet count of a bundle at chest height, plus a visual check of notes held up (~90 s).
9. 92-111 s — notes re-banded/arranged into two bundles on the desk.
10. 112-123 s — storage: both bundles picked up, cabinet opened (116 s), bundles placed inside (119 s, 122-123 s).
11. 124-128 s — cabinet closed and locked/handle checked; no cash left on the desk.
12. 129-142 s — operation recorded on the laptop (typing).

No client-related steps (ID check, handover across the desk, signature) occur because no client is present. Nothing in the video deviates from the expected order: inspect bag -> open -> extract -> empty-check -> count by machine -> count by hand -> store -> close safe -> register.

### Detection cues

- Person count: exactly one person inside the room for the whole video; zero persons on the client side of the desk. Any "client present" / "two clients" / "unauthorized person" logic must require a person INSIDE the room (floor region), not silhouettes behind the glass partition.
- Role assignment must come from behaviour/position relative to the equipment (counting machine, cabinet, laptop), not from "near side / back to camera": here the cashier is on the far side facing the camera.
- Collection-bag check: green bag object held at chest height for ~40 s (1-40 s) with both hands at its top; a small tool in hand (8-15 s); bag inverted overhead (35-39 s). A bag-inspection detector should fire on "bag lifted and held >3 s with hands at the closure" before any cash appears on the desk (cash first visible at 31 s).
- Counting: hands at the top (hopper) of the black counting machine for >=2 s on three occasions (52-54, 63-65, 75-76 s) and hands at its front stacker (55, 66, 104 s); manual count = bundle held at chest height with repeated thumb/finger motion for ~7 s (84-91 s). Total counting activity ~60 s between first cash on desk (31 s) and storage (119 s).
- Storage: cabinet door state (open 116-125 s, closed 126 s onwards) combined with the cashier remaining seated within reach of the cabinet; cash leaves the desk at 114 s and enters the cabinet, not a pocket or bag.
- End state: desk empty of cash from 115 s; cashier at the laptop 129-142 s.
- Absence cues: no smartphone-sized object in hands at any time; no ID-card-sized object presented; no hand crossing the desk from the near side.

- 41-44 s: cashier turns her back to the camera and bends down at the cabinet while the two cash bundles lie on the desk, then stands with both hands at her head/chest. A naive "cash on desk + cashier not in chair" rule would raise Наличные оставлены без присмотра, and a "hands to body" rule might suggest pocketing — but she is at the desk, within 1 m of the cash, and her hands are empty (the bag was stowed, the cash is visible on the desk).
- 0-44 s: cashier is standing, not seated, for 44 s. "Cashier left workplace" (Кассир покинул рабочее место) must be based on the person leaving the desk zone, not on standing vs sitting; she never leaves the desk area.
- 116-126 s: cabinet door open for ~10 s with bundles in the cashier's hands near her chest — looks like "safe open / cash near body"; it is the normal storage step with the cashier present.
- 84-91 s (and 49-51, 60-61, 106-107 s): bundles held close to the torso at chest height — resembles concealment but is the manual count.
- 5 s and 36 s: cashier leans to the laptop while holding the bag — not phone use; no handheld device.
- 8-16 s: small dark tool in the right hand near the bag closure — could be mistaken for a phone by a weak detector; it is scissors-like and used on the bag, then laid on the desk (visible 16-33 s).
- Background persons behind the glass (1-5, 20-24, 36-44, 128-142 s): never enter; must not trigger Посторонний в зоне кассира or Наличные двух клиентов одновременно.
