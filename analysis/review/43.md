### Normal protocol observed

This clip defines a *cashier-only cash count* (no client transaction). Ordered steps:

1. **0 s** — cashier seated alone at her workplace, banknotes in hand at the desk centre.
2. **1–3 s** — banknotes placed into the counting machine (left arm to the machine top, hand at the hopper for ~2 s, then withdrawn upward at 3 s).
3. **4–6 s** — cashier waits, hands clasped at the desk centre, looking ahead / toward the door side; does not leave the seat.
4. **7 s** — looks at a small item (note or slip) in her left hand.
5. **8 s** — reaches to the machine and takes the notes back.
6. **9–12 s** — banknote-by-banknote handling by hand at the desk centre, gaze on the notes (manual verification after the machine).
7. **13–16 s** — both hands at the machine's front unit / display, a white slip visible (reading the result / second pass — not resolvable at 1 fps).
8. **17–18 s** — stack held in both hands at the centre, leafed through.
9. **19–23 s** — notes counted down onto the desk one by one into a pile at the centre; cashier stays seated with her hands on / next to the pile when the clip ends (23.4 s).

Moments a naive detector could wrongly flag (all legitimate):

- **1–3 s and 13–16 s** — the cashier's arm is extended to the far right of the desk toward the counting machine, which in the image sits directly in front of the grey cabinet/safe → "reaching toward the safe" / "safe interaction" false positive. Her hands stay at desk height on the machine; the cabinet door stays closed.
- **0 s and 7 s** — a small light/green rectangular item held close to the body with the gaze down → `phone_at_workplace` look-alike. The item is thin and banknote/card sized and is the stack that goes into the machine at 1 s.
- **4–6 s** — cashier sits back, hands clasped, looking away from the desk toward the door side → "cashier distracted / not attending" heuristics; she is simply waiting for the machine.
- **19–23.4 s** — banknotes lying on the desk → `cash_left_unattended` false positive if the rule does not require the cashier to be absent; she is seated at the desk with her hands on / next to the pile.
- **Whole clip** — the near-side blue chair (the cashier's seat in the briefing layout) is empty → any seat-based "cashier absent" rule fires for 23 s (`cashier_left_during_service` / `cash_left_unattended` false positive), and a seat-based role rule would classify the cashier as a client handling cash with no cashier present.
- **Whole clip** — cash is handled without any document being shown → `no_id_check` false positive if the rule does not condition on a client being present. There is no client transaction here, so the ID step is not applicable.
- **Whole clip** — cash is never handed over → `issue_without_count` / `accept_without_count` must not fire on counting alone.

### Detection cues

What an automatic system should see for this normal cashier-only count:

- **Person count = 1 for the whole clip**; one stationary person bbox at the far side of the desk; no door-region motion, no entry/exit tracks.
- **Role from behaviour, not seat**: the cashier is the person whose hands repeatedly enter the counting-machine ROI (camera-right end of the desk) and who sits next to the laptop. In this video she faces the camera, so face-visible does not mean client.
- **Counting-machine use**: left-arm extension events into the machine ROI with the hand at the top of the machine, ~2–3 s long (1–3 s), a short retrieval reach (8 s), and a ~4 s dwell of both hands at the machine's front unit (13–16 s). Gaze toward the machine during these windows.
- **Hand count**: both hands close together at the desk centre with a small green object between them, repetitive small finger/wrist motions, head tilted down; durations of 3 s or more (9–12 s, 17–18 s). Counting-down pattern at 19–23 s: one hand holds the stack while the other places single notes, producing a growing green blob on the desk centre.
- **Cash present on the desk while the cashier bbox persists** (19–23.4 s) → attended, not a violation. Unattended requires cash ROI occupied **and** no cashier bbox for several seconds.
- **No handover**: no second person, no hand crossing the desk midline from the near side, no object transfer between two people.
- **Safe never opened**: cabinet door edge unchanged in all frames; the cashier's hands never go behind her toward the cabinet.
- **No phone**: the handheld objects are banknote-sized, held flat between two hands and are fed into the machine; no screen glow, no single-hand upright grip near the face.
