- 17–22.7 s: the bag stays on the desk without being put in the safe before the clip ends — "Наличные оставлены без присмотра" must not fire because the cashier is seated and has both hands on the bag the whole time.
- 20 s: the collector bends down toward the desk to sit — body moving toward the desk can look like a reach.

### Detection cues

- Role assignment: the seated person with back to the camera at the near side of the desk is the cashier; the person who enters through the top-left door and stays beyond the far desk edge is the client/collector. Use the desk edge polygon (not a vertical line) as the zone boundary; feet/hip position decides the zone, not hands.
- Collection scenario trigger: a bag-like object (green cloth, roughly A4) carried by the entering person. This should switch the expected protocol from "banknote counting" to "document check + bag/seal inspection".
- Document check: a small bright rectangular object (card) travels client-hand -> desk centre -> cashier-hand (9 s), stays with the cashier for >= 3 s with hand(s) raised to shoulder/face height (10–14 s), then travels back (15 s). Gaze direction is not needed; the raised-hands pose with the object is sufficient.
- Bag inspection: bag object on the desk surface AND both cashier wrists within the bag's bounding box for >= 3 s (17–22.7 s here). Precedence matters: document check (10–14 s) happens BEFORE the bag is released to the cashier (16–17 s).
- Negative checks that stay quiet: no banknote-shaped objects on the desk; counting-machine region motionless; safe door region unchanged (closed); cashier's seated pose unchanged (no stand-up event); only one person inside the room polygon besides the cashier.
- Duration cues: entry-to-document handover ~5 s; document examination ~5 s; bag inspection >= 5.7 s and still ongoing at the end of the clip.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- Client handing an ID card (ordinary cash operation) vs. collector handing the accompanying document — same motion and object; both are correct behaviour.
- Cashier examining a card near her face vs. phone use — distinguish by object colour/size (white card vs. dark slab), by the fact that the object came from the client's hand and is returned to the client, and by two-hand raised pose lasting only a few seconds.
- Cashier gripping the top of a bag (seal inspection) vs. opening a bag to take cash out — the latter would be followed by banknotes visible on the desk and a counting motion / counting-machine run; here nothing comes out.
- Collector standing at the left end of the desk next to the counting machine vs. an unauthorized person in the cashier zone — the collector's feet and body stay beyond the far desk edge; only his bag overlaps the machine in 2-D.
- Collector sitting down late (20–21 s) vs. "second client arrives" — it is the same person; track identity across the standing->sitting transition.
- Cashier reaching across the desk to return the document (15 s) vs. cashier reaching toward the client zone to take cash — context (document in hand, nothing counted) decides; by itself the reach is normal.
- Bag left on the desk at the end of the clip vs. cash left unattended — cashier present and in contact with the bag; the clip simply ends before storage.
