2. 2.0–3.5 s — Cash is withdrawn with her body in front of the open door; the open-door interval is short (door open ≈ t 1.5 – 4.5, about 3 s).
3. 3.5–5.0 s — Safe door closed immediately after the withdrawal; hand on the handle/lock at 5.0 (locking gesture).
4. 5.0–8.0 s — Cash carried directly from the safe to the workstation, in the hand and in view the whole time: no pocket, no bag, no detour, no stop, no second person.
5. 8.0–9.0 s — Cashier seated at the workstation before any handling of the cash starts.

### Detection cues

- Safe-door state: closed = flat light-grey front; open = a light-grey door panel protruding towards camera-left with a dark interior (t 2–4). Normal pattern: "door open" fully overlaps "cashier bounding box within arm's reach of the safe" and lasts ≈3 s. Risk pattern (not here): door open with no person within reach, or door open while the cashier is seated/walking away.
- Cash object: small, light/white rectangular bundle; first visible in the right hand at ~6 s (waist height), at desk centre between both hands 9–12 s, on top of the counting machine (hopper ROI, far-left corner of the desk) from 13 s to the end. Its track is continuous: safe -> hand -> desk -> machine.
- Counting-machine use: hand(s) enter the machine ROI (far-left desk corner) for ≥2 s and a bright stack appears on the hopper and persists; it is preceded by ≥2 s of hands-together-at-desk-centre (manual handling). Either one alone (manual leaf-through or machine loading) already satisfies "пересчёт".
- Role assignment: in this clip the cashier is the person who (i) touches the safe at 0–5 s and (ii) touches the counting machine at 12–15 s. She sits on the FAR side facing the camera, so neither chair side nor facing direction can be used as the cashier cue; use interaction with the safe/machine and track identity from the first frame.
- Person count: exactly one person inside the room for all 19.6 s; the door region shows no opening; silhouettes behind the glass (above/left of the door frame) must be masked out.
- "Waiting for machine" state (15–19.6 s): seated, hands clasped and empty on the desk, gaze towards the machine ROI, stack present on the machine.
- Durations: safe open ≈3 s; cash in hand before counting ≈3 s (6–9 s, all of it walking/sitting); manual handling ≈3 s; machine loading ≈2 s; waiting ≥4.6 s.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- 0–6 s: cashier standing at the open safe with the chair empty. A seat-occupancy rule ("cashier not at the desk while cash is out") would fire "Кассир покинул рабочее место до окончания обслуживания клиента" or "Наличные оставлены без присмотра". Neither applies: no client is being served and the cash is in her hands. Require a client in frame for the first rule and cash on the desk with no person within reach (or an open safe with nobody at it) for the second.
- 0–5 s: a standing person inside the cashier's zone next to the safe could be mis-labelled "Посторонний в зоне кассира" if the cashier identity is taken from the seated position. Track the person from frame 0; the same track sits at the workstation at 8 s.
- 6–8 s: cashier walking with cash in hand — resembles "leaving with cash". Her distance to the desk decreases every frame and the walk ends seated at the workstation at 8 s.
- 9–12 s: head down, hands together on the desk holding a small light object — the posture resembles phone use ("Использование телефона на рабочем месте"). Disambiguate by object colour/shape (light bundle vs dark slab), by the hand-over-hand leafing motion, and by the object being placed into the counting machine at 13 s.
- 12–15 s: hands move to the extreme left edge of the desk (hopper ROI) — resembles cash moving out of view / pocketing / "hiding". Exclude the machine ROI from any "cash disappears" logic.
- Dark figures behind the glass door (≈5–7 s, ≈12–19 s): must not count as a second client ("Наличные двух клиентов одновременно") or as an unauthorized person.
- No ID shown in the clip, but there is no client: "Операция без предъявления документа" must require a client-side person and a handover event, otherwise this clean clip is flagged.
- Cash handled with a counting machine but no client: "Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта" / "Выдача наличных без пересчёта в присутствии клиента" both need a second person and a handover; none occurs here.
