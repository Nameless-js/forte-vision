### Detection cues

1. Safe-door state: the safe's front face is a flat uniform grey rectangle when closed; when open, a lighter grey door panel protrudes from the left edge of the cabinet body (towards the desk/camera) and a dark interior gap appears. A small two-class classifier (closed/open) on the fixed safe ROI, or a change detector on that ROI versus a closed-door template, is enough; the camera is fixed.
2. Cashier-to-safe distance: track the cashier (person bbox / pose). Define "at the safe" as the bbox overlapping or within about 0.3 m (roughly 60 px at this scale) of the safe ROI. Violation condition: `safe_open AND NOT at_safe` persisting for at least 1.5–2 s. In this clip it starts at about 4.5 s and lasts to the end (7 s or more).
3. Sequence prior: person walks to safe → door opens → arm(s) enter the safe ROI → person leaves the ROI with hands occupied → door still open. The "hands occupied with a light-coloured bundle" cue (3.5–5 s) confirms cash was taken.
4. Hand-count cue (confirms cash, not the violation): fanned light green/white notes in both hands at 10 s; bundle lifted to the hopper of the counting machine at 11 s.
5. Durations: door open 2.0 s → end (9.6 s or more); unattended from 4.5 s → end (7 s or more); the walk from safe to chair takes about 1.5 s (3.5 → 5.0 s).
