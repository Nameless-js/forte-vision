### Candidate type

- id: `two_clients_cash_mixed`
- Russian name: «Наличные двух клиентов одновременно» (второй клиент допущен к кассе, пока наличные предыдущей операции не убраны)
- Reasoning: the label is placed exactly at the moment client 2 walks in (56.5–57.0 s), not at any hand-over, counting or door event of client 1. The only thing that makes that moment abnormal is the state of the desk: the beige stack deposited by client 1 (counted at 25–43 s) was never put into the safe/cassette, client 1 is gone, and now a second client enters and is served. The cashier then takes the second client's cash and lays it next to the first client's cash (68–70 s). The whole sequence is the textbook "cash of two clients on the desk at the same time / next client admitted before the previous operation's cash is put away" scenario, and the organisers chose the first instant at which it is visible — the second client's entry.
- Precise t_event: 57.0 s. First pixels of client 2 appear at the door edge at 56.5 s; at 57.0 s he is fully inside the room walking toward the desk with client 1's cash lying on the desk. (If a rule needs "two bundles physically on the desk", that becomes true at 66.0 s when the cashier takes the blue bundle while the beige one is still out, and is unambiguous at 68.0 s when both lie side by side.)
- Alternatives considered:
  - `cash_left_unattended` («Наличные оставлены без присмотра») — partial overlap only: the cash of a finished/abandoned operation lies out on the desk (44 s to end), but the cashier never leaves her seat and never opens the safe, so the "unattended" condition is not met. Some rule sets would phrase this video's fault as "наличные предыдущего клиента не убраны до начала обслуживания следующего"; that is the same event as the chosen category, viewed from the storage side.
  - `accept_without_count` («Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта») — client 2's bundle is accepted at 66 s and only looked at (67 s) before being put down (68 s); no counting until the video ends at 72.7 s. Inconclusive because the operation is cut off, and it is 9+ s after the label, so not the labeled event.
  - `other` — "operation not closed / cash not stored before the client left" (44–53 s): the counted cash stays on the desk while client 1 takes his card and leaves. This is the precondition of the labeled event rather than a separate labeled moment.
  - Rejected: `no_id_check` (both clients show a card that the cashier holds up and inspects: 15–16 s and 62–64 s), `unauthorized_in_cash_zone` (client 2 keeps to the far side; nobody crosses to the cashier's side or touches the safe), `cashier_left_during_service` (she never stands), `phone_at_workplace` (no phone in the cashier's hands; the small dark object at 58–59 s is in the client's hand), `issue_without_count` (no cash is ever handed to a client), `collection_bag_unchecked` (no bag, no collector), `handover_bypassing_transfer_device` (no tray in the scene).

### Detection cues

- State to track: `cash_on_desk` = a banknote-bundle detection on the cashier's side of the desk (beige or blue rectangular stack, roughly 1/8 of the desk width, lying between the cashier's chair and the counting machine). In this video it is true continuously from 44 s to 72.7 s. It must be cleared only by a "store" event: cashier's hand carrying the bundle to the safe/pedestal zone, a drawer/cassette, or the safe door opening. None happens here (safe ROI never changes).
- Person tracks on the client side: client 1's track ends at the door ROI at 52–53 s (walking away, back to the camera) while `cash_on_desk` is true; a NEW track (different clothing: dark jacket vs white shirt) appears in the door ROI at 56.5–57.0 s and reaches the client chair at 60 s. Flag at the door crossing of the new track when `cash_on_desk` is still true and no store event occurred since the previous client left.
- Confirming cue: a second bundle (different colour) is handed from the client's hand across the desk (65–66 s) and placed within a few centimetres of the first one (68 s); two bundle detections on the cashier's side at once = hard confirmation.
- Pose cues: cashier seated with hands at the keyboard (44–46, 54, 70–72 s) while a bundle is on the desk = "not stored"; her hand-to-safe / hand-to-pedestal trajectory never occurs.
- Durations: client-side empty gap 53–57 s (4 s) with cash out; new client at the desk from 57 s onward (15+ s); two bundles co-present 66–72.7 s.
- Door ROI (top-left glass door) is the trigger; client chair ROI (far side) is the confirmation that the newcomer is a client being served, not someone passing by.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- Normal client turnover: client leaves, next client enters a few seconds later — identical person-track pattern, but with the cash put in the safe/cassette first. The rule must be conditioned on `cash_on_desk`, not on two client tracks alone.
- Client 1 standing up (49–50 s) while the cash is still on the desk is the same operation, not a second client; only a new track through the door counts.
- The cashier re-touching the stack at 47 s and 55–56 s looks like a "pick up to store" start; the bundle never leaves the desk, so a store event needs the hand+bundle to travel to the safe zone, not just a touch.
- Receipts, the white ID cards (13–48 s, 61–64 s) and papers are small white rectangles on the desk; a banknote-bundle class must use colour/thickness and provenance (came from the client's hands or the counting machine) to avoid counting a card as cash.
- A collector entering while the desk is being cleared, or the cashier handling her own change float with a client present, would trigger the same object/person logic; the ownership of the bundle (which client handed it over) has to be remembered by the tracker.
- The same client leaving and immediately coming back (forgotten item) would be a new door crossing; re-identify by clothing colour before flagging.
- Hand counting (36–43 s) and the brief look at the blue bundle (67 s) are both "bundle in cashier's hands"; only the first has a leafing motion over several seconds.
