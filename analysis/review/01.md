### Candidate type

- **id:** `issue_without_count` — «Выдача наличных без пересчёта в присутствии клиента»
- **Reasoning:** the labeled second coincides exactly with the start of the only cashier -> client cash handover in the video (fanning at 22.0 s, arm extension at 22.5 s, contact at 23.0 s, completed at 23.5 s). Before the handover there is no banknote-by-banknote counting by hand at any time (11–13 s and 20–22 s are holding/arranging/fanning motions lasting 1.5–3 s, not a count). The only machine step is 14–19 s: the stack is placed on top of the machine, lies there motionless for ~5 s and is lifted from the same place; no notes are seen passing into a stacker, so a completed machine count is not evidenced. Nothing else happens at 22 s: no one enters, the cashier stays seated, the safe is closed, no phone, the ID was checked earlier (7–8 s). The organisers chose the handover second, so the violation is about the issue itself being done without a (visible) recount in front of the client.
- **Alternatives:**
  - `other` — "cashier retains part of the notes": at 22.0 s the notes are split (light fan in the left hand, yellowish item in the right), only the light notes are visibly handed over, and a yellowish item of the same look is in her right hand near her chest at 25.0 s. If that item is a banknote, the staged story could be partial retention of the client's cash rather than a counting violation. Its identity and its path between 22.5 and 24.0 s (left hand occluded) cannot be established from the frames.
  - `accept_without_count` — «Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта»: cash was accepted hand-to-hand at 10 s and never counted leaf by leaf; if the machine step is judged as not a count, the acceptance was also uncounted. However the organisers' timestamp is 22 s, not 10–14 s, and the cash was not stored but returned, so this is less likely to be the labeled event.
  - `handover_bypassing_transfer_device` — excluded: there is no tray/transfer device in the scene, and the identical hand-to-hand acceptance at 10 s is not labeled.
  - `no_id_check` — excluded: the card is handed over and inspected at 6–8 s before any cash moves.
- **Precise t_event:** **22.5 s** — first frame in which the cashier's arm is extended across the desk with the notes towards the client (the handover motion). Contact is at 23.0 s and the client holds the notes from 23.5 s; a detector keyed on hand-to-hand contact should fire at 23.0 ± 0.5 s. The 22.0 s label corresponds to the last frame before the arm extension (notes fanned at face level).

### Detection cues

- Track the cash object (stack of notes: light rectangular blob with printed texture, roughly 15–30 px wide in the 464x848 frame) through the states client-hand -> cashier-hands -> machine-top -> cashier-hands -> client-hand. The violation is the transition cashier-hands -> client-hand (cash crosses the desk midline from the near side to the far side while held by the cashier, then is released into the client's hand).
- Handover signature: the cashier's right wrist travels from her body to the far half of the desk (reach > ~50 % of desk depth) within ~0.5 s; the client's wrist moves towards the same point; both wrists within a few px of each other for 0.5–1 s; afterwards the cashier's wrist returns empty and the cash blob stays on the far side.
- Pre-condition to check in the ~15 s before the handover: (a) hand count — stack held low over the desk with repetitive thumb/leaf motion for at least 3–5 s; absent here; (b) machine count — notes leave the hopper region and appear in the stacker, cashier looks at the machine display; not visible here (blob stationary on the machine top from 16 to 19 s, lifted from the same spot). If neither (a) nor (b) is found, flag the handover.
- Timing cue: stack-in-cashier-hands-after-machine (20.0 s) to stack-in-client-hand (23.5 s) is about 3.5 s; hold-at-face-level about 1.5 s. Counting episodes in normal videos should be several times longer.
- Context cues available in this video: ID card on the desk since 9 s (so `no_id_check` is not the reason), safe closed, cashier seated throughout, client seated throughout.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- Returning the client's ID card or a receipt/slip: same arm extension by the cashier, but the object is a small white card/paper, not a note stack; check the object class and size before contact.
- Accepting cash from the client (10 s here): same hand-to-hand contact at mid-desk but in the opposite direction; the direction must be decided from which hand held the stack before contact.
- Cashier reaching to the counting machine (14–15 s and 19–20 s): a long right-arm extension across the desk towards the far-left end, with cash in hand — it ends at the machine, not at the client's hand, and the client's hands stay at the desk edge.
- Showing the counted stack to the client or fanning the notes briefly to check them (20.5–22 s): looks like the "hold-up" phase here; it is only a violation if the handover follows without a count.
- A correctly machine-counted issue (notes visibly run through the machine right before the handover) must not be flagged.
- The client's dark rectangular object at 9 s and the background person's object behind the glass (20–25 s) can trigger a phone detector; both are outside the cashier's workplace.
