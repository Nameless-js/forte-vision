### Normal protocol observed

This clip defines the normal "cashier leaves the workplace with no client" sequence:

1. 0–5 s — cashier works at the laptop; no client, no cash on the desk, counting machine idle.
2. ~5.5 s — laptop screen goes dark while her hands are on the keyboard (interpretation: session locked/closed before leaving; only the brightness change is observed).
3. 6–7 s — cashier stands up with empty hands.
4. 8–10 s — brief (≤2 s) handling/check at the safe box; hands empty before and after; the safe front looks closed when she steps away at 10–11 s.
5. 10–12 s — walks to the door with empty hands; nothing left on the desk.
6. 12–13 s — opens the glass door and leaves.
7. 13–17.3 s — workplace unattended but with no cash out, no open safe, no client at the desk or entering.

Moments a naive detector could wrongly flag:
- 6.5–13 s: "cashier stands up and leaves" — the motion signature of cashier_left_during_service, but there is no client (visitor side empty, nobody enters 0–13 s).
- 8.5–10.5 s: cashier's hand in the safe ROI — the signature of a safe-storing step (accept_without_count) or of leaving the safe open; here the hands are empty and the box looks unchanged.
- 13–17.3 s: empty desk with no cashier — the signature of cash_left_unattended, but no cash is visible and the safe is closed.
- 0–6 s: silhouettes behind the glass door could be counted as a waiting client or an unauthorized person; they stay outside.
- 13–17.3 s: the cashier's own pink top behind the glass may be re-identified as a new person entering.
- The black handset-like desk device next to the laptop resembles a phone for a weak detector; it is never lifted.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- Stand-up-and-leave (6–13 s) is visually identical to "Кассир покинул рабочее место до окончания обслуживания клиента"; only the absence of a client distinguishes them. Rules must condition on a client track / an open transaction, not on the cashier's absence alone.
- Empty desk after 13 s looks like "Наличные оставлены без присмотра" except for the absence of cash on the desk and the closed safe; the rule needs a cash-object detection or an open-safe detection, not just "desk without cashier".
- The reach towards the safe (8–10 s) resembles the storing step of "Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта"; here no cash was received and the hands are empty.
- Figures behind the glass door (0–6 s; cashier herself 13–17 s) resemble a waiting client or "Посторонний в зоне кассира"; they are outside the room.
- The fixed black desk device beside the laptop resembles a smartphone ("Использование телефона на рабочем месте"); it never moves and is never held.
