### Normal protocol observed

1. 0–1 s — cashier at her workplace, desk clear, machine empty, safe closed.
2. 2–6 s — client enters through the door, walks along the far side (client side) of the desk and sits on the far chair; he never crosses to the cashier's side.
3. 7–8 s — client presents a document: small white card passed across the desk surface; cashier takes it with her right hand at 8 s.
4. 9–11 s — cashier visibly inspects the document, holding it up at chest/face height for about 3 s while the client waits. The ID check happens BEFORE any cash appears.
5. 10–12 s — client retrieves cash from his jacket chest pocket and places it on the desk centre (12–13 s). Direction: client → cashier (acceptance of cash).
6. 14–16 s — cashier picks the notes up and counts them by hand (two-handed note-by-note leafing) in front of the seated client, ~2–3 s.
7. 17–18 s — cashier places the counted bundle into the counting machine hopper (machine verification); notes remain visible in the hopper to 20 s.
8. 19–20 s — cashier returns to an upright seated posture, client remains seated; no cash is put away, the safe is not opened and the clip ends before any storage/return step.

Moments a naive detector could wrongly flag:
- 2–6 s: a person walking through the door and along the far side of the desk — a crude "person in room / near desk" rule could fire `unauthorized_in_cash_zone` (Посторонний в зоне кассира); he stays on the far (client) side the whole time.
- 7–8 s: the client's hand crosses the desk centre to hand over the card — a desk-centre zone-crossing rule would fire `unauthorized_in_cash_zone`; it is a document handover on the desk surface.
- 9–11 s: the cashier holds a small white rectangular object near her face for ~3 s — a phone classifier could fire `phone_at_workplace` (Использование телефона на рабочем месте); it is the client's ID card (white, thin, received from the client 1 s earlier).
- 10–11 s: the client reaches inside his jacket — could be read as concealment; he is taking out the cash that appears on the desk at 12–13 s.
- 12–13 s: hand-to-hand / desk-surface cash handover — `handover_bypassing_transfer_device` is not applicable because there is no tray in the scene.
- 13–14 s: notes lie on the desk for ~1 s between the client's release and the cashier's pickup — `cash_left_unattended` (Наличные оставлены без присмотра) would be a false alarm, the cashier is seated and reaching for them.
- 17–18 s: the cashier turns her torso to the LEFT and stretches her arm away from the client — a "cashier turns away / reaches to a drawer" rule could misfire; the reach goes to the counting machine (left), not to the safe (right).
- 19–20 s: cash stays in the machine hopper with the cashier seated beside it — not unattended, and not `accept_without_count` (Приём наличных без полистного пересчёта) because the hand count at 14–16 s precedes it.
- 0–20 s: a person behind the glass partition — a person counter without a room mask would count a second person and could trigger `two_clients_cash_mixed` (Наличные двух клиентов одновременно).

### Detection cues

- Role assignment: the track that is seated on the near chair (back to camera, between the laptop and the counting machine) for the whole clip is the cashier; the track that enters through the top-left door and settles on the far chair is the client. Mask out the area behind the glass partition.
- Zones (frame coordinates): near half of the desk = cashier zone; far half = client zone; left end of the desk = counting machine; right of the desk = safe. A hand crossing the desk centre line is a handover, not a zone intrusion, as long as the body stays on its own side.
- ID step: a small white card-sized object moves client-hand → desk centre → cashier-hand (7–8 s) and is then held near the cashier's face for ≥2 s (9–11 s). Required to occur before the first cash appearance.
- Cash step: light-coloured banknotes first appear in the client's hand (12 s), rest on the desk centre (13 s), then are in the cashier's hands. Expected pattern for a correct acceptance: (a) two-handed leafing above the desk lasting ≥2 s (14–16 s) and/or (b) the bundle placed into the machine hopper (17 s) — both with the client seated in view.
- Order check: ID shown (8–11) → cash handed (12–13) → hand count (14–16) → machine count (17–20). Storage into the safe, a drawer or a pocket before (a)/(b) would indicate `accept_without_count`; cash handed with no visible ID step would indicate `no_id_check` (Операция без предъявления документа).
- Negative cues present here: the cashier never stands, the safe door never opens, no phone-shaped dark object appears, only one client is at the desk, the counting-machine hopper goes from empty to loaded exactly once.
- Durations observed: entry-to-seated ~4 s; ID inspection ~3 s; cash on desk between hands ~1 s; hand count ~2–3 s; machine placement ~2 s.

### Look-alike normal actions (FP risks)

- Handing a document vs. handing cash: both are "client's hand crosses the desk centre with a small object"; distinguish by colour/shape (white card vs. coloured notes) and by what the cashier does next (holds it up to look vs. leafs through it).
- Holding an ID card near the face vs. using a phone: both are small rectangles near the face; the card is white/light, thin, arrives from the client's hand 1 s earlier, and is put down on the desk afterwards.
- Reaching to the counting machine (left) vs. reaching to the safe/drawer (right): same arm extension and torso twist, opposite direction.
- Client reaching into his jacket to take out cash vs. pocketing something: distinguish by what appears afterwards (notes come out and go onto the desk).
- Cashier bending/turning toward the machine at 17–18 s vs. cashier leaving: the hips stay on the chair, the chair does not move.
- Person behind the glass vs. a second client: never crosses the door plane.
